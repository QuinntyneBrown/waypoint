Bible, code and mentorship — built to grow leaders.

Two tracks and a mentor for every youth. Here's exactly what a young person learns, builds and walks away with.

Bible & Leadership Coding for Kingdom Mentorship
Track 1

Bible & Leadership

Youth grow in the Word and learn to lead like Jesus — anchored in Mark 10:45, the servant leader.

“For even the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve.”Mark 10:45

What youth learn

  • Who Jesus is and what following him means
  • Their identity and worth in Christ
  • Servant leadership as a way of life
  • How to lead and facilitate a small group
  • How to share their own story with others

Track 2

Coding for Kingdom

Real, practical tech skills — so youth can build, create and serve their neighbourhood with what they make.

What youth learn

  • Computational thinking with Scratch
  • How websites are structured with HTML
  • Styling and layout with CSS
  • Building and shipping a first real project
  • Presenting their work at showcase night

Every youth, a mentor

Mentorship

Content changes lives when it comes with relationship. Every young person is matched with a trained mentor for the length of their cohort.

Weekly check-ins

Mentors meet youth each week to review progress, pray and encourage.

Reflection reviews

Youth submit reflections on each lesson; mentors respond with real feedback.

Extra support

When a youth is at risk of falling behind, mentors step in early.
