Privacy

Privacy & safeguarding

Waypoint works with young people, so protecting their privacy and safety is central to everything we do.

What we collect

When you apply, we collect the details on the application form — the applicant’s name, age, neighbourhood, and, for under-18 applicants, a guardian’s contact information — so we can review the application and, with consent, welcome you into a cohort.

How we use it

We use your information only to run the program: reviewing applications, arranging consent, and supporting your journey. We never sell personal information.

Safeguarding

Guardian consent is required before a minor participates, and everyone who works with youth completes a screening and reference check. Payments are handled by an external PCI-compliant provider — we never store card details.
