# Project Governance

## Principles

Waypoint is maintained openly, with decisions guided by participant safety, safeguarding, privacy, accessibility, correctness, maintainability, and the project's purpose: helping young people grow as leaders who love Jesus. Delivery speed and technical convenience do not override these responsibilities.

Requirements and acceptance criteria are part of the product contract. Authentication, authorization, consent, auditability, and participant-data boundaries must be enforced on the server and supported by test evidence.

## Roles

### Contributors

Anyone who submits an issue, design, requirement, documentation, code, review, or other project improvement is a contributor. Contributions must follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

### Maintainers

Maintainers triage issues, review and merge changes, manage releases, moderate project spaces, and steward security, safeguarding, and community standards. The repository owner, [Quinntyne Brown](https://github.com/QuinntyneBrown), is the current lead maintainer.

## Decision making

Day-to-day decisions are made through issues and pull requests. Maintainers seek rough consensus using documented requirements, participant impact, test evidence, and architecture decisions. The lead maintainer makes the final repository decision when consensus cannot be reached.

Significant technical or product decisions should be recorded in [docs/DECISIONS.md](docs/DECISIONS.md). Security-sensitive, safeguarding-sensitive, or personally identifying details may remain private.

## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainers may invite contributors who demonstrate sustained, constructive participation; sound technical judgment; reliable reviews; and consistent adherence to project values. Maintainer access may be removed for inactivity, security reasons, or violations of project policy after reasonable notice when circumstances permit.

## Changes to governance

Governance changes are proposed and reviewed through a pull request. Material changes should remain open long enough for active contributors to respond.
