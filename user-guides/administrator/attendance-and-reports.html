Program administrator / 12

Attendance, the progress board and reports

This page is about running the program week to week and then reading it back out: putting sessions on the calendar, marking who turned up — including in a church basement with no signal — watching progress build, and exporting the numbers. It ends with roster import, the one way to bring an existing group into Word Up in bulk.

Program Administrator Mentor WU-UG-12

Scheduling sessions

Choose Calendar in the Program group. As a Program Administrator the page is headed Program calendar with the line Schedule sessions across cohorts and open their attendance rosters. A Mentor sees the same screen headed Calendar with Schedule your cohort sessions and open their attendance rosters.

Three views sit under the date range: Month, Week and Agenda. Month and week draw a grid with each session as a link; agenda lists them as cards with a button reading Take attendance, or Edit attendance if attendance has already been recorded. An empty agenda reads No sessions in this agenda range.

Beside the views are Calendar cohort (defaulting to All cohorts, or All my cohorts for a Mentor) and three navigation buttons, Previous, Today and Next. Sessions with no venue show Location TBD.

Creating a session

The New session panel sits to the right of the calendar. It takes:

Field Notes
Title Up to 200 characters. Required.
Cohort Starts at Choose cohort. Required, and it is what fixes the allowed date range.
Mentor Starts at Choose mentor. Required.
Date Clamped to the selected cohort's term — the date picker will not offer days outside it.
Time Required.
Minutes 1 to 1440. Optional.
Location Up to 256 characters. Optional.
Repeat weekly through the cohort term A tick box. On, it creates one session per week from your start date through to the term end, inclusive, and never beyond it.

Schedule session shows Scheduling… and then reports either Session scheduled. or N weekly sessions scheduled through the cohort term.

A session must sit inside its cohort's term

A session outside the cohort's start and end dates is refused with The session must fall within the cohort term. The date picker already constrains you, so this normally only bites when the cohort has no dates set, or when you are working around the picker. Set the cohort's term first — see Cohorts, curriculum and settings.

Two client-side checks fire before the request is even sent:

  • Complete the title, cohort, mentor, date, and time.
  • Enter a valid date and time.

Mentors are further restricted: Mentors may only schedule sessions assigned to themselves. and This cohort is not assigned to you.

Taking attendance

Choosing a session anywhere on the calendar opens its roster. The title is the session title, the eyebrow is the cohort, and the subtitle is the full date, start time and location. Calendar and History links sit at the top right.

The tallies

Five counters run across the top: n of N marked, then present, late, excused and absent. They update live as you mark people, and they are the reliable way to check your work.

Marking

Each young person is a row with four buttons — Present, Late, Excused, Absent — and a text field labelled Staff note for name, up to 1000 characters. Two shortcuts sit above the list: All present marks everybody present in one go, and Clear unmarks everybody.

Preview limitation

The four status buttons look identical whether or not they are chosen — there is no highlight, no fill and no border change on the one you picked. Only assistive technology reports the choice, through aria-pressed. Use the tally counts above the list to confirm what you actually marked; do not trust the buttons to show you.

A line at the foot reads Unsaved changes or Changes saved. Save attendance stays disabled until every young person on the roster is marked — a partial roster cannot be saved. It shows Saving… while it works and the page then reports Attendance saved. An empty roster reads No active youth are on this session roster.

Navigating away with unsaved marks asks You have unsaved attendance changes. Save your attendance draft before leaving? Select OK to save the draft, or Cancel to stay. Choosing OK stores your marks locally and restores them when you return.

Volunteers see less of this screen

A Volunteer taking attendance may mark only Present or Absent, and may not add notes about a young person at all. See Run today's session.

When somebody else got there first

Attendance rows carry a version. If the roster changed on the server since you loaded it, saving is refused rather than silently overwriting: This attendance record was changed by someone else. Reload and try again. On screen you see Attendance changed on the server. Your selections remain here; review and reconcile them. — your marks are not thrown away. Reload, look at what changed, and re-apply what is still right.

Taking attendance offline

Attendance is the one part of Word Up that works without a connection, and it was built that way deliberately: rosters get taken in basements, gyms and church halls.

When you are offline the app shows its standing banner — You’re offline — changes will save when you reconnect. — and Save attendance keeps working. Instead of going to the server, your save is queued on the device and the page reports Saved offline — N attendance updates waiting to sync. A badge above the roster reads N pending sync.

When the connection comes back, the queue drains on its own. You will see Syncing N attendance updates… and then All attendance changes synced. Nothing is asked of you.

Resolving a sync conflict

One case does need you. If somebody edited the same session on the server while you were offline, your queued change cannot simply be applied. The page reports A queued attendance change conflicts with a newer server edit. Review it before saving again. or, if the rest of the queue drained first, A queued attendance change needs review before it can sync.

An amber panel then appears on the session, and it gives you exactly two ways out.

  1. Read the panel

    It is headed Choose which attendance to keep and explains A newer server edit conflicts with your queued change. Review before choosing.

  2. Keep yours, or keep theirs

    Apply my changes re-bases your queued marks onto the current server versions and pushes them, overwriting the other edit. The page reports Applying your reviewed attendance changes… while it works.

    Keep server attendance throws your queued change away and reloads what the server holds, reporting Kept the newer server attendance and removed the offline conflict.

    What you should see

    Either way the amber panel disappears and the pending-sync badge drops. If it does not, the queue still holds something — check the message line under the roster.

Offline is attendance only

Nothing else in Word Up works offline. There is no offline lesson reading, reflection drafting, quiz taking or submission, and the queue lives in this browser on this device — signing in elsewhere will not find it.

Attendance history

Choose Attendance in the Progress group. For you the page is headed Attendance administration under the eyebrow Program administration; a Mentor sees Attendance under Mentoring. Both read Review turnout by session or attendance patterns by youth. An Open calendar button sits top right.

The card is headed Attendance history and has two views, By session and By youth. Both share the same three filters — Cohort (defaulting to All cohorts, or All my cohorts for a Mentor), From and To — applied with Apply filters.

By session

Columns are Session (title and date/time), Cohort, Present, Marked (as marked / roster total), Rate, Status and Action. The status pill is one of saved (green), needs review (amber) or low turnout (red). The action link reads Edit if anything is marked and Take attendance if nothing is. Empty, the table reads No sessions match these filters.

By youth

Columns are Youth, Attended (as attended / total), Late, Excused, Absent, Rate and Trend. Anyone below the threshold carries a red Below 70% badge next to their name — that is the signal to look at, and it is the same threshold the at-risk work uses. Empty, the table reads No youth match these filters.

Exporting

Export CSV and Export PDF sit at the top right of the card and export the view you are looking at, with the filters you have applied. There is no other export format and no custom report builder.

Preview limitation

The By session / By youth tab strip does not respond to arrow keys. Use Tab to move between the two tabs. The same applies to the reports hub tab strip described below.

The progress board

Choose Progress Board. The page is headed Progress board under the eyebrow Learning outcomes, with the line Every youth across every published module in the selected track.

Note the word published. Draft and in-review modules do not appear here at all, because they do not exist for young people. If a module is missing from the board, check its publish state before you check anything else.

Two filters sit above the board — Cohort and Track — with Refresh, Export CSV and Export PDF beside them. The export buttons read Exporting… while they run and are disabled when there is nothing to export.

Live updates

A badge at the top right reads Connecting live updates… and then turns green as Live updates on. While it is green the board updates itself as young people complete work — you do not need to press Refresh. If it stays on Connecting live updates…, the live channel has not come up and Refresh is your fallback.

Reading the matrix

The first column is Youth, then one column per published module, then Quiz average and Overall. A footer row gives Cohort average per module and overall. Each cell shows that young person's state on that module.

The three empty states

No cohorts available

Create a cohort before opening the progress board. You have not built any cohorts yet.

No published modules

Track has no published modules to show yet. Choose another track or publish curriculum content. The track exists but nothing in it is live.

No youth are assigned to this cohort.

The board is drawn, with modules, but the cohort is empty. Place some young people in it.

The reports hub

Choose Reports. The page is headed Reports hub under the eyebrow Program insight, with the line Explore organization, youth, cohort, attendance, and staff activity from one filtered view.

The shared filters

One filter card governs the whole page: Report filters — Every overview and staff metric uses this exact scope. It takes Date from, Date to, Report cohort (All cohorts), Report track (All tracks) and Report mentor (All mentors), applied with Apply report filters. A backwards date range gives Date to must be on or after date from.

Under the filters, two facts about what you are looking at: Updated date and time and N youth records. Quote the first when you circulate an export, because reports are a snapshot, not a live document.

The four views

Tab What it shows
Overview Six KPI tiles — Active youth, Average completion, Attendance, Retention, Cohorts and Active mentors — then Attendance breakdown (present, late, excused, absent) and Cohort completion as a bar per cohort. With nothing in scope: No report data for these filters and Change the date or program filters and try again.
Youth report Pick somebody under Youth and choose Generate youth report. You get three rings — Curriculum progress, Average quiz score and Attendance rate — then Module completion, Quiz scores, Attendance, Mentor-assessed competencies, Mentor notes summary and Badges.
Cohort report Cohort report selection plus an optional Compare with, which defaults to Program average / previous term. Then Generate cohort report gives enrolment and status counts, three KPIs, Module completion, Youth summary and Comparisons.
Staff activity Caseload, check-ins, and reviews within the current report filters. One card per mentor with those three figures, their load-balance badge and their target band. Empty: No staff activity for these filters and Change the report scope and try again.

Staff activity is Program Administrator only. Mentors can reach the reports hub and the youth and cohort reports, but the staff-activity data is refused to them. It is the one view on this page that is not shared.

The youth report deliberately stops short of the private material: Private note details are not included in this summary. You get how many check-ins happened and the latest engagement label, never the content of a mentor's private notes or a young person's prayer requests.

Exporting

Export CSV and Export PDF at the top of the page export whichever view you are on, with the current filters. They read Preparing CSV… or Preparing PDF… while they run and confirm with CSV export prepared for the current report and filters.

Announcements

Choose Announcements in the Program group. The page is headed Announcement administration with the line Send program or cohort updates and follow recipient read-through.

The left card is Compose announcement — Choose exactly who should receive this update. Two decisions decide who gets it, and they are independent.

Audience

Organization or Cohort. Choosing Cohort reveals a picker starting at Choose a cohort; leaving it empty gives Choose a cohort for this audience.

Recipients

Youth, Youth + guardians, Guardians, Mentors or Everyone. This narrows the audience by role, so Cohort plus Guardians reaches the guardians of one cohort and nobody else.

Title is up to 200 characters (Enter a title up to 200 characters.) and Message up to 4000 (Enter an announcement message.).

Sending now or later

Delivery is a choice between Send now and Schedule for later. Choosing the second reveals Send date and time, which must be in the future — Choose a send date and time in the future. The button changes to match: Send announcement or Schedule announcement, and Saving… while it works. The outcome is Title was sent. or Title was scheduled.

The right-hand list is Sent and scheduled — Read statistics refresh from authoritative recipient records. Each entry carries a Sent or Scheduled badge, its audience and recipient group as badges, the send or schedule time, and two figures: n of N read under Recipient opens, and a percentage under Aggregate read rate. Empty, it reads No announcements yet with Sent and scheduled updates will appear here.

Importing a roster

Choose Import Roster. The page is headed Import roster under the eyebrow People, with the line Bring an existing roster into Word Up in three reviewed steps: upload, map, and import. A standing notice sets expectations: Export a Google Sheet as CSV first. The source sheet stays untouched, and only rows marked Ready will create youth accounts.

There is no live connection to Google Sheets or anything else. Import is a one-off CSV upload, reviewed before it commits.

  1. 1 · Upload CSV

    The field is Roster CSV and the rule is stated under it: CSV only, up to 2 MB. The file is validated before it is sent. Validation happens in your browser, so a bad file never leaves the machine.

    What you should see

    A summary showing the file name, the row count, the column count and the size, with a Remove file button. If instead you get an error, it is one of these five:

    • Choose a .csv file. Other file types are not accepted.
    • Choose a CSV file that is 2 MB or smaller.
    • Choose a .csv file with a CSV content type.
    • The CSV must contain a header row and at least one data row.
    • Every CSV column header must be unique.
  2. 2 · Map columns

    CSV column → Word Up field. Leave unneeded columns as Skip. The table shows your CSV column, a Sample value from the data, and a Word Up field picker offering Name, Email, Cohort, Mentor, Track, Status, Consent and — Skip column —. Word Up guesses from your headers; check the guesses rather than trusting them.

    Two mapping rules are enforced before you can go on:

    • Map exactly one column to Email before previewing. Email is the identity; without it there is nothing to create an account against.
    • Map each Word Up field at most once.

    Preview roster reads Checking rows… while it works.

  3. 3 · Preview

    Review every row before confirming the import. Four counts sit at the top:

    ready

    Will create an account. This is the only count that does anything.

    needs consent

    A minor with no valid guardian consent record. Shown in the table as Missing consent.

    duplicates

    That email already belongs to somebody in Word Up.

    invalid

    The row cannot be read as a person.

    The table then lists every row with Name, Email, Cohort, Mentor, Track and its Import status, with a specific reason underneath where there is one. The button reads Import N ready people and is disabled when the ready count is zero.

  4. Confirm

    A dialog opens headed Confirm roster import, stating exactly what is about to happen: Only N ready rows will create an account. Duplicates, missing-consent rows, and invalid rows will remain unchanged. Cancel or Escape backs out; Confirm import proceeds, showing Importing…

    What you should see

    A card under the eyebrow Roster saved, headed Import complete, with four figures — imported, duplicate skipped, needs consent, invalid — and a View people button. The imported figure should match the ready count you confirmed.

Only ready rows create accounts. Nothing else in the file is touched, changed or partially applied. That means a failed import is safe to retry: fix the source spreadsheet, export again, and run the three steps a second time. The rows that already imported will simply come back as duplicates.
