Volunteer / 14

Run today's session

As a volunteer you have one job in Waypoint: mark who turned up. The whole app is built around doing that in a church hall with poor signal, and around showing you as little about each young person as the job needs. This page is short because the role is.

Volunteer W-UG-14

What you can and cannot see

Your account shows less than a mentor's or an administrator's, and that is a safeguarding decision rather than a gap in the build. Knowing the boundary up front saves you looking for screens that are not there.

You can You cannot
See the sessions you have been assigned to See any other session, cohort or roster in the program
See the names of the young people on that roster See anything else about them — no progress, no reflections, no contact details, no guardian information
Mark each name Present or Absent Mark Late or Excused — those exist in the product, but only mentors and administrators may use them
Save that attendance, online or offline Write a note about a young person anywhere in the app

The attendance screen states the first half of this itself, under its heading: Only names and Present/Absent attendance are shown.

There is no note field, on purpose. If you need to pass on something about a young person — a concern, an injury, a conversation — tell the mentor or program director running the session. Do not try to record it in Waypoint; there is nowhere for it to go, and a volunteer note is deliberately not part of the record.

Your sidebar has exactly three items: Today, Attendance and Schedule. On a phone the same three appear along the bottom of the screen instead.

Preview limitation

The Attendance item in the navigation does not open a roster. Choosing it — in the sidebar or the bottom bar — returns you to Today. The only working way to a roster is Today and then Take attendance, which is the route this page uses throughout. The notification bell in the top bar behaves the same way: it returns you to Today rather than opening notifications.

The Today screen

Today is where you land when you sign in. It is headed Today’s session with the line Your assignment for today, at a glance.

When you are assigned to something today, one card shows:

  • the session title;
  • the day, date and start time, with the length in minutes where one is set;
  • the location, where one is set;
  • 12 on the roster — how many young people you should expect;
  • a Take attendance button, which is the way in to the roster.

When you are not assigned to anything today, the card is replaced by No session today and the line You have no assigned session today. Check your schedule for what’s coming up. with a View schedule button. That is the expected screen on a day off — it does not mean anything is wrong with your account.

Take attendance

Take attendance opens the roster, headed Take attendance with Today’s session above it and a Back to today link beside it. Every name on the roster is one row with two buttons.

  1. Start from everyone present

    All present marks every name at once, which is usually faster than working down the list. Then change the few who are missing to Absent. Clear unmarks everybody if you want to start again.

  2. Mark each name

    Each row has a Present button and an Absent button. Choosing one replaces the other, and you can change your mind as many times as you like before saving.

    What you should see

    The three tallies above the list move as you work: 9 of 12 marked, then 8 present and 1 absent. Below the list, Unsaved changes appears while you have anything not yet saved.

  3. Save the whole roster

    Save attendance stays unavailable until every name is marked — the count in the first tally has to reach the total. Attendance is saved for the session as a whole, not name by name.

    What you should see

    The button reads Saving… while it works, the line below becomes Changes saved, and a note reading Attendance saved. confirms it.

Preview limitation

The Present and Absent buttons have no visible selected state — the one you chose looks exactly like the one you did not. Do not try to check your work by looking at the buttons. Use the tallies above the list instead: the marked count tells you how far you have got, and the present and absent counts tell you whether the split is right.

If you try to leave the roster with unsaved marks, a browser confirmation box appears carrying exactly this: 

You have unsaved attendance changes. Save your attendance draft before leaving? Select OK to save the draft, or Cancel to stay.

OK keeps your marks so they are still there when you return; Cancel leaves you on the roster. Neither answer sends the attendance — only Save attendance does that.

Working offline

Attendance is the one part of Waypoint built to work with no connection. When you go offline, a banner appears across the top of the app reading You’re offline — changes will save when you reconnect. Carry on marking the roster exactly as before.

What changes is what Save attendance does. Instead of reaching the server it puts the whole set of marks in a queue on this device, and tells you so. These are the messages you will see, word for word.

Saved offline

Saved offline — 1 attendance update waiting to sync. Your marks are safely queued. The count rises if you save more than once, and reads attendance updates for more than one.

Syncing

Syncing 2 attendance updates… Waypoint noticed the connection return and is sending the queue on its own. You do not press anything.

Done

All attendance changes synced. The queue is empty and the session is recorded. This is the message to wait for before you close the app.

Needs a decision

A queued attendance change needs review before it can sync. or A queued attendance change conflicts with a newer server edit. Review it before saving again. Something else changed the same session while you were offline — see the next section.

While anything is queued, a pending sync badge sits above the roster with the number of updates waiting. The queue lives on the device you saved from and belongs to your account, so finish a session on the same phone or tablet you started it on.

Offline covers attendance only

Nothing else in Waypoint works without a connection. If the queue still shows updates waiting at the end of a session, get back into signal or onto wi-fi and leave the app open until you see All attendance changes synced.

When someone else has already saved

A mentor or an administrator can record attendance for the same session you are working on. If they did so after you went offline, your queued marks and theirs disagree, and Waypoint will not guess which is right. It stops and asks you.

A warning appears on the roster headed Choose which attendance to keep, with this explanation: 

Someone saved this session after you went offline. Review the current roster, then apply your queued selections or keep the newer server attendance.

Two buttons sit under it, and they do opposite things.

Apply my changes

Your queued marks win. Waypoint reloads the current roster, reapplies what you saved offline on top of it, and sends it, reporting Applying your reviewed attendance changes… Use this when you were in the room and they were not.

Keep server attendance

The other person's version wins and your queued update is discarded, reporting Kept the newer server attendance and removed the offline conflict. Use this when someone with a fuller picture has already recorded the session.

Read the roster before you choose — it is showing the saved attendance as it currently stands. If you are not sure which is right, ask the mentor running the session rather than guessing; whichever you choose replaces the other outright.

The same clash can happen while you are online, without a queue involved. Then Save attendance simply reports Attendance changed on the server. Your selections are still here; reload the roster and reconcile them. Nothing you marked is lost; reopen the roster from Today and mark it again against what is now recorded.

Your schedule

Schedule in the sidebar lists the sessions you are assigned to, headed Your schedule with the line Sessions you’re assigned to help with. Each one shows its title, its status, the day, date and start time, and the location where one is set.

With nothing assigned you see No upcoming sessions and You have no upcoming sessions assigned yet. We’ll show them here once you’re scheduled. Volunteers are assigned to sessions by a program administrator — there is nothing to sign up for here, so if a session you expected is missing, ask them.
