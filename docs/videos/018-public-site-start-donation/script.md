# 018 · Starting a donation

## Welcome

Some supporters want to help by giving. This short video shows how a visitor starts a monthly donation on the public site, and why Waypoint never touches anyone's card details.

[pause 2s]

## The donation page

Sam Whitfield opens the public site and follows the donate link from the get involved page. The page is called, back a young leader. It explains that a monthly gift helps a young person belong, grow, and lead.

The form is short. Your name, and email, and both are required. Below them is a row of monthly amounts. Twenty dollars a month, fifty dollars a month, and one hundred dollars a month. The button that continues to secure checkout stays dimmed until Sam picks an amount.

Just under the amounts, a note says that payments are processed securely by an external provider that meets card industry standards, and that Waypoint never sees or stores card details. There is no place on this page to type a card number.

[pause 2s]

## When something is missing

If Sam enters an email that is not valid and leaves the name empty, Waypoint does not go on. Each field explains the problem right beneath it. Enter your name. Enter a valid email address. Nothing is recorded until both are fixed.

[pause 2s]

## Confirming

Sam fills in the name and email, chooses fifty dollars a month, and presses continue to secure checkout. Waypoint records the pledge, which is the name, the email, and the monthly amount. No card data is part of it.

The page then says, thank you. It repeats what Sam chose, fifty dollars monthly, and says to finish the donation with the secure provider, which opens in a new tab. A button, continue to secure provider, takes Sam there.

The monthly amounts shown on this page are still a product decision, so the program may change them.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Anyone can start a donation without signing in. A name, an email, and a monthly amount are all it takes. The page never asks for card details, because the payment is finished with an external secure provider. A clear thank you page shows what was chosen.
