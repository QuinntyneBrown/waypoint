# 089 · When a guardian needs to sign off

## Welcome

Waypoint is built for young people, and some of them are minors. Before a minor can take part, a parent or guardian has to give consent. This video shows what a young person sees when that consent is missing, and the two ways Waypoint helps them fix it.

[pause 2s]

## When consent is missing

Meet Imani Clarke. She is a youth in the Fall cohort. Her guardian, Ruth, had given consent, but then withdrew it. The next time Imani tries to finish a lesson, Waypoint stops her.

She does not see a blank page or a confusing error. She sees a page headed, a guardian needs to sign off. Above it, a small label says, consent needed. The text explains the situation in plain words. Before she can start lessons, a parent or guardian needs to give participation consent. It only takes a minute. She can send them a secure link, or have them sign now.

Nothing about the page blames Imani. It tells her what is missing, who can fix it, and what she can press.

[pause 2s]

## Two ways to get the signature

Under the explanation there are three buttons. The first is, notify my guardian. Imani presses it, and Waypoint sends a secure consent link by email to her guardian. The page confirms it right away. It says that a secure consent link was sent by email, and it shows the address with most of the letters hidden, so the page does not give the full address away.

The second button is, my guardian can sign now. This is for the moment when Ruth is sitting right next to her. Waypoint signs Imani out, and takes the next sign-in to the guardian consent page. Ruth can then sign in with her own account and give consent herself.

The third button simply says, sign out, for anyone who wants to leave.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

A minor without consent is stopped at one clear page, not a dead end. The page names the problem in plain language. Imani can notify her guardian by email, or have the guardian sign right then. Either way, the consent itself is given by the guardian, never by the young person.
