# 026 · Managing an account

## Welcome

People join a program, change roles, forget passwords, and sometimes leave. This video shows how Pat Morgan, the program administrator, manages all of that from one person's record, and how Waypoint makes the serious actions hard to do by accident.

[pause 2s]

## The Account tab

Pat opens the record of Sam Whitfield, a volunteer, and chooses the Account tab. It shows his status, his role, when he was last active, and when the account was created. Below are two groups of actions. Access and role. And Account lifecycle.

Sam has forgotten his password, so Pat presses Send password reset. A green message confirms that a reset link has gone to his email address. The action is written to the audit log.

[pause 2s]

## Changing a role

Later, Sam is asked to help as a mentor. Pat picks Mentor in the role list and presses Change role. A window opens, headed Change account role. It names the person, and the old and new role. A warning says that this staff role grants access to young people's records and protected program data, that the change takes effect immediately, and that all current sessions will be revoked. Only when Pat presses Confirm role change does anything happen, and the change is audit logged.

[pause 2s]

## Deactivating and removing

When someone steps back, Pat presses Deactivate account. The window explains that the person will be signed out and unable to sign in, and that progress, attendance, notes and reporting records are retained. It also says Pat can reactivate the account later. After Pat confirms, the page shows the status as Deactivated, and a Reactivate account button takes its place.

Removing a person is different. The window is headed Permanently remove person, and its warning says the removal cannot be undone. Access is revoked and the person leaves active use, but progress and reporting records are kept. To go ahead, Pat must type the person's full name, Sam Whitfield, into a box. Only then does the Permanently remove button work.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Everything lives on the Account tab. A password reset emails a link to the person. A role change warns first, and takes effect straight away. Deactivation is reversible and keeps every record. Removal cannot be undone, and asks for the person's name. Records are retained either way.
