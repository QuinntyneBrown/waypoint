# 027 · Managing cohorts

## Welcome

A cohort is a group of young people who move through the program together. This video shows how Pat Morgan, the program administrator, creates a cohort, adds youth to it, and keeps its details up to date.

[pause 2s]

## Creating a cohort

Pat opens Cohorts from the menu and presses New cohort. A window called Create cohort opens. It asks for a cohort name, a curriculum track, and a lead mentor. Those three are marked as required. Pat also enters a start date, an end date, and a meeting location. Here the cohort is called the Fall cohort, and it meets at the community hall.

If something is missing, the form says what to fix. Enter a cohort name. Choose a curriculum track. Choose an active lead mentor. And the end date must come after the start date.

When Pat presses Create cohort, a card for it appears on the page. The card shows the track, the lead mentor, the number of members, the location, and the dates.

[pause 2s]

## Adding youth

Pat opens the new cohort. The detail page lists the cohort details, and below them, the members. A new cohort has none yet, and the page says so. In the panel called Add members, Pat chooses an existing young person, Amara Bell, and presses Add youth.

Waypoint tells Pat that the youth was added to the cohort and enrolled in its track. Amara now appears in the member list, with a View record button, and the member count goes up to one. If the person is not in Waypoint yet, an Invite a youth button leads to the invitations page instead.

[pause 2s]

## Editing a cohort

Plans change. Pat presses Edit cohort. The window says that changes are recorded in the audit log. Pat can update the cohort name, the lead mentor, and the meeting location, then press Save changes.

[pause 2s]

## When the list will not load

If the list of cohorts cannot be loaded, the page shows an error with a Retry button. If there are no cohorts at all, the page says No cohorts yet, and offers the New cohort button as the first step.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

A cohort needs a name, a track, and a lead mentor. Youth are added from the cohort page, and the member count follows. Edits are recorded in the audit log. Errors offer a retry, and an empty list offers a first action.
