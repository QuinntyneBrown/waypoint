# 068 · Exchanging messages

## Welcome

Young people in the program need a safe place to talk to their mentor. This video shows how messages work in Waypoint: a private thread between one youth and one mentor, and a broadcast that a mentor can send to a whole group.

[pause 2s]

## A private thread

Meet Amara Bell, a youth in the cohort, and Grace Adeyemi, her mentor. Amara opens the messages page. It says, your private conversation with Grace Adeyemi. Grace's welcome note is already there, asking Amara to message her whenever she needs support.

Amara types, I finished today's lesson, and presses send message. The message lands in the thread with its time. There is also a small badge that says, live updates on. That means Amara does not need to refresh the page. New messages appear as they arrive.

[pause 2s]

## The mentor inbox

On her side, Grace opens her messages page. She sees an inbox, with one conversation for each youth, and a search box to find a youth or a cohort. She opens Amara's conversation and reads the message. She replies, great work, see you at the next session.

Amara's page updates by itself, and shows a short notice that a new message has arrived from her mentor.

Messages are only readable by the two people in the thread. The system also cleans message text, so nothing harmful can be slipped in. And a person can only message people their role allows.

[pause 2s]

## Broadcast to my group

Sometimes Grace has the same news for everyone. Beside the inbox is a button, broadcast to my group. She picks her cohort, and writes her reminder. Before anything is sent, Waypoint asks her to review it. The review says, send one private message to one youth. She can see exactly how many people will receive it.

When Grace confirms, each youth receives the reminder as an individual personal message, in their own thread with her. Youth never see who else received it. Amara's thread now shows the reminder, right below the earlier conversation.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Every conversation is private between a youth and their mentor. Messages arrive live, with a small live updates badge. A broadcast is sent as one personal message per youth, after a review step that shows the recipient count.
