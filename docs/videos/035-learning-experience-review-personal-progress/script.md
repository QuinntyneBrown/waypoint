# 035 · Reviewing your own progress

## Welcome

A young person should be able to see how they are doing without waiting for someone to tell them. This video shows the My progress page, where a youth sees their own numbers in one place.

[pause 2s]

## The overview

Amara opens My progress from the side menu. The page says, the full picture of how you are growing, and it has three tabs. Overview, Quizzes, and Attendance.

The overview starts with four numbers. Overall progress, quiz average, lessons done, and attendance. For Amara, overall progress is fifty percent, her quiz average is eighty two percent, she has done one of two lessons, and her attendance is fifty percent.

[pause 2s]

## Leadership competencies

Below the numbers is a section called leadership competencies. Each competency shows a level and a percentage. Amara sees serving others, at the level proficient, at seventy five percent. These levels come from assessments made by her mentor, so they are a mentor's judgement and not something she scores herself.

[pause 2s]

## Quizzes

On the Quizzes tab is her quiz history. It is a table with the quiz, the module, the score, and the result. Her quiz shows eighty two percent and passed. The history lists the scores that count, so a quiz that is still waiting for mentor approval does not show a number.

[pause 2s]

## Attendance

On the Attendance tab, she sees each session with its date and her status. One session says present, and another says absent. The rate at the top, fifty percent, is the same figure she saw on the overview.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

My progress has three tabs. The overview shows overall progress, quiz average, lessons done and attendance. Competency levels come from the mentor. Quiz history shows score and passed or not, and attendance shows each session. It is her own data, in one place.
