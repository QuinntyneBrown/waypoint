# 095 · Working without a connection

## Welcome

Program sessions do not always happen where the internet is strong. This video shows how Waypoint tells a person that their device is offline, what keeps working, and how it shows changes that are waiting to be saved.

[pause 2s]

## Taking attendance

Sam Whitfield is a volunteer. He opens today's session and the take attendance page. The roster is loaded, and he marks everyone present. The page shows each young person in the session, with a present button and an absent button, and only names and attendance are shown. Everything looks normal.

[pause 2s]

## The device goes offline

Then the connection drops. At the top of the page, a dark banner appears. It says, you are offline. Changes will save when you reconnect.

Waypoint notices the lost connection on its own. The page Sam was already looking at does not disappear. Anything that was loaded stays on screen, and he can keep working. He marks one young person absent and presses save attendance.

[pause 2s]

## Changes waiting to sync

Waypoint cannot reach the server, so it keeps the change safely on the device. A message says, saved offline, one attendance update waiting to sync. Near the roster, a small yellow badge reads, one pending sync.

That badge counts the changes that have not reached the server yet. Sam never has to guess whether his work counted.

[pause 2s]

## Back online

When the connection returns, the banner goes away on its own. Waypoint sends the waiting change, and the message changes to, all attendance changes synced. The pending badge disappears.

Sam did not have to press anything extra, and the change was not saved twice.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Offline is always visible, with a banner at the top. What was already loaded stays usable. Waiting changes are counted in a pending badge. When the connection returns, the banner clears, and the changes sync by themselves.
