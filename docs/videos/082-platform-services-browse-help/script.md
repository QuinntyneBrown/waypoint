# 082 · Finding answers in the help center

## Welcome

Not every question needs a person. Waypoint has a help center where anyone who is signed in can look up an answer on their own. This video shows how it works, and what happens when the answer is not there.

[pause 2s]

## Opening the help center

Meet Amara, a youth in the Fall cohort. She wonders why one of her lessons is locked. She opens the help center, and sees a page headed, how can we help? Under it, a line says, search answers written for your Waypoint role.

The answers are grouped by topic. Getting started. Learning. Account and privacy. Each topic holds a few questions. Amara taps the one that asks, why is a lesson or quiz locked? The answer opens right there. It tells her that some activities have prerequisites, and that she can open the curriculum to see which module or activity to finish first.

[pause 2s]

## Written for your role

The help center is written for the role of the person asking. Grace is a mentor. When she opens the same page, she sees different questions. She sees, how do I review a youth submission? And, how do I take attendance? Amara does not see those, because they are not her work.

That keeps every list short, and it keeps each person looking at answers that apply to them.

[pause 2s]

## Searching

At the top there is a search box. Amara types the word, badges. The page narrows to the one question that matches, where can I see feedback and badges? The search also looks at the answer and at a few related keywords, not only at the question.

If she types something that matches nothing, the page does not go blank. It says, no help articles found. Try a different word, or contact the support team.

[pause 2s]

## Still need a hand

At the bottom of the page there is a card that says, still need a hand? It explains that she can send a private request and the program team will follow up, and it has a button, contact support. That button takes her to the support form, which is the subject of a later video.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

The help center lists questions by topic, and each person sees only the questions written for their role. Search narrows the list as you type. When nothing matches, the page says so and points to support. And contact support is always one button away.
