# 091 · When a page is not for your role

## Welcome

Waypoint shows each person only what their role needs. Sometimes someone still ends up at a page that belongs to a different role, perhaps from an old bookmark or a shared link. This video shows what happens then, and why it is safe.

[pause 2s]

## The wrong door

Meet David Osei. He is a youth. Somehow he opens the address of the people directory, a page that only program staff can use.

Waypoint does not show him the page, and it does not show him a blank screen either. The server refuses the request, and Waypoint opens a clear page headed, you do not have access to that. A small label above it says, error four oh three.

The text explains the reason in plain words. This area is for a different role. If he thinks it is a mistake, he can switch to the right account, or head back to his home.

[pause 2s]

## Nothing leaks

Notice what the page does not contain. There are no names, no counts, and no hint of what is in the people directory. The server is the one that refuses, and the page only explains the mismatch of roles.

[pause 2s]

## Two ways forward

There are two buttons. The first is, back to your home. David presses it and lands on the youth home, the place made for his role.

The second is, switch account. It signs him out, so that someone who has an account for the right role can sign in with it.

Either way, David is never stuck. Both buttons lead somewhere useful.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

A page for another role is refused, and the refusal comes from the server. The explanation is plain and the page leaks nothing from the area that was refused. Back to your home and switch account are always on offer.
