# 013 · Browsing current cohorts

## Welcome

Before a young person applies to Waypoint, they usually want to know one thing. Who else is in this, and where? This short video shows the public About page, where any visitor can browse the current cohorts without signing in.

[pause 2s]

## The About page

Meet Ruth, a guardian who has never used Waypoint. She is looking at options for a young person in her care. She opens the public site and chooses About in the top menu. The page explains that Waypoint helps young people grow in faith, skills, and leadership, alongside a dedicated mentor and a neighbourhood cohort.

Under the heading, find your community, she sees the list of current cohorts. No account is needed, and nothing on the page asks her to sign in.

[pause 2s]

## What each cohort shows

Each cohort is one card. The card gives the cohort's name and a small badge with its status, for example upcoming or active. Below that are three plain facts. The neighbourhood where the cohort meets. The dates it runs, from the first day to the last. And the lead mentor, shown by name.

Notice what is missing. There are no names of young people, no guardians, and no member lists. The public page only shows what a visitor needs to choose a cohort. Waypoint builds this page from a small set of safe fields, so private information cannot slip onto it.

The status also comes from one place. Waypoint works it out from the cohort's start and end dates, so the same cohort shows the same status on every public page.

[pause 2s]

## When nothing is listed

Sometimes there is no cohort to show. Then the page does not go blank. It says, no cohorts are currently listed, and adds that she can still apply, and the team will share upcoming options. Two buttons at the bottom of the page, apply now and explore the programs, always give her a next step.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

The About page is open to everyone. Each cohort card shows its status, neighbourhood, dates, and lead mentor, and nothing about the young people in it. The status is the same on every public page. If no cohort is listed, visitors can still apply.
