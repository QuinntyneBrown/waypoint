# 076 · Safeguards for young people

## Welcome

Waypoint serves young people, some of them minors, so it goes beyond ordinary privacy rules. This video walks through four safeguards. Consent before participation. Private notes staying private. Media only with permission. And roles that do not open every door.

[pause 2s]

## Consent before participation

Imani Clarke is working through a lesson. Her guardian, Ruth, has signed the participation consent, so everything works normally.

Now suppose Ruth withdraws that consent. The next time Imani tries to complete a lesson, Waypoint does not let it through. She is taken to a screen headed, a guardian needs to sign off. The check is made by the server each time Imani does something protected, so it is not only a check at sign in. When Ruth gives fresh consent, Imani carries on in the same session.

[pause 2s]

## Private stays private

Mentors write notes about check-ins with young people. Those records are kept, so the program can be accountable, and the director can see them according to policy. But what a guardian sees is limited.

On the guardian progress page, a card called Shared mentor updates shows only the check-ins the mentor chose to share. A check-in marked private is simply not there. Ruth sees the goals and next steps that were shared, and nothing else.

[pause 2s]

## Media only with permission

Photos and video of a young person may be used only where media consent is on record, with the matching option, and has not been withdrawn. The option the guardian chose decides what may be used. Withdrawing media consent stops further use.

[pause 2s]

## Roles do not open every door

Being a mentor or a volunteer does not, by itself, give access to every young person's record. Each role sees only what its scope allows. A volunteer account, for example, does not become a window into a young person's private details just because the account exists.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Participation consent is checked on every protected action. Private check-in notes never reach guardians. Media is used only with the right permission on record. And a role gives access only to what that role needs.
