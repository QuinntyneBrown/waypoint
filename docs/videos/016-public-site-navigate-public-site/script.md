# 016 · Finding your way around the public site

## Welcome

The public site is the front door of Waypoint. Anyone can open it, with no account, and find their way to the right place. This short video shows how a visitor gets around, on a computer and on a phone, and where signing in fits.

[pause 2s]

## The top menu

Meet Pat Morgan, who runs the program. Pat opens the public site and lands on the home page. The page opens without any sign in screen in the way.

Across the top is the menu. On the left are four links. Programs, About, Get involved, and Contact. On the right are two buttons. Sign in, and Apply. These are the main things a visitor may want to do, and they are on every public page.

The home page itself also has two buttons in the middle. Apply now, and explore the programs.

[pause 2s]

## The footer and the privacy page

At the bottom of every page is a footer with the same four links, and one more, Privacy. The privacy page explains what the program collects and how it uses it, and it sets out the safeguarding rules. Anyone can read it without signing in.

If a visitor follows a broken link, they do not get a blank page. They see, we couldn't find that page, with two buttons. Back to home, and Apply.

[pause 2s]

## On a phone

Many visitors will arrive on a phone. On a narrow screen the page rearranges itself. The text stays readable, the two main buttons stack one above the other, and nothing needs sideways scrolling.

[pause 2s]

## Signing in

The public site shows only public information. It does not show anything about young people, mentors, or guardians. When someone who already has an account presses sign in, Waypoint takes them to its sign in page, which is a separate application, and they sign in there.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Anyone can open the public pages without signing in. The top menu and the footer are on every page, and the main buttons are always in reach. A missing page shows a helpful message. The layout adapts to a phone. Sign in leads to the separate sign in page.
