# 102 · The youth report

## Welcome

Before a check-in, a review, or a conversation with a guardian, someone needs the whole picture of one young person. The youth report puts it on one page. This video shows what is in it.

[pause 2s]

## Generating the report

Pat Morgan, a program administrator, opens the reports hub and chooses the youth report tab. There is one menu, labelled Youth. Pat picks David Osei, who has been falling behind, and presses generate youth report.

[pause 2s]

## The headline

The report opens with David's name, his cohort, and his mentor. Under it are three rings. Curriculum progress shows how much of the curriculum he has completed. Average quiz score shows how he does on quizzes. And attendance rate shows how often he has been there. Three rings, and the story is already clear.

[pause 2s]

## The detail

Below the rings are cards for the detail. Module completion lists each module, with the percent complete and how many lessons are done out of the total. Quiz scores lists each quiz with its score. Attendance counts sessions as present, late, excused, or absent.

Mentor assessed competencies lists each competency with the level the mentor chose. And the mentor notes summary says how many check-ins the mentor has recorded, with the date and engagement label of the latest one. It deliberately leaves out the private notes themselves, and the page says so in plain words. Private note details are not included in this summary.

The last card is badges. Each badge David has earned is listed with its date.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

One menu, one button, one page for one young person. Three rings give the headline, and the cards give modules, quizzes, attendance, competencies, a mentor notes summary, and badges. Private notes never appear in the report. Mentors see only the reports for their own youth.

That completes the tour of reporting and data.
