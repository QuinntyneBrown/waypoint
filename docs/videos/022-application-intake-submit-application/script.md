# 022 · Submitting an application

## Welcome

Every young person's journey with Waypoint starts with an application. It is a public page. Nobody needs an account to use it, and a parent, a guardian, or a young person can fill it in. This video follows Ruth Clarke as she applies on behalf of her daughter Imani, who is fifteen.

[pause 2s]

## The form

Ruth opens the apply page on the public site. The heading says join a cohort, and a line underneath says the program is open to young people aged twelve to eighteen.

The first question is who is filling this out. Ruth can choose, I am applying for myself, I am a parent or guardian, or I am referring someone. She picks parent or guardian. Next she names the cohort she is interested in. This one is required. Then she enters Imani's first name, last name, age, and neighbourhood. She can pick a track, or leave it on no preference yet, and she explains in her own words why Imani is interested.

[pause 2s]

## Age and guardian details

The form checks the age as soon as it is typed. If someone enters twenty, a red message appears right under the field. It says applicants must be aged twelve to eighteen inclusive. The form tells the person what is allowed, rather than just saying no.

When Ruth enters fifteen, a new section appears. It is headed parent or guardian, and it says it is required for applicants under eighteen. Ruth gives her name, her email, and her phone number. If the applicant were eighteen, this section would not appear.

[pause 2s]

## Consent and privacy

At the bottom there are two boxes. The first confirms that a parent or guardian consents to participation. The second says the person has read the privacy notice. Both are required.

If Ruth presses submit application before ticking them, nothing is sent. A short message appears under each box. Please acknowledge participation consent, and please acknowledge the privacy notice. The same kind of field message appears under any required field she has left empty. She ticks both boxes and presses submit again.

[pause 2s]

## Application received

The page changes to a confirmation. It says application received. It thanks Ruth, says Waypoint will review the application and reach out with next steps, and makes one thing clear. Imani does not have an account yet. An account is set up only after she is accepted into a cohort.

Behind the scenes, the application is recorded as new and goes to the program administrator's queue. The server checks and cleans every piece of text again, so the page is not the only line of defence.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

The form is public, and no account is needed. It asks who is filling it in, the young person's details, and why they are interested. Applicants under eighteen need guardian details. Both the participation consent box and the privacy box must be ticked. A successful submission is recorded as new, and no account exists until the application is approved.
