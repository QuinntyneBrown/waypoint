# 008 · Recovering a locked account

## Welcome

Sometimes a person simply cannot remember their password. After a few wrong tries, Waypoint steps in to protect the account. This video shows what that looks like, and how the person finds their way back in without waiting on anyone.

[pause 2s]

## Five misses

Meet Imani. She is a youth in the program, and she is trying to sign in from a computer she does not use often. She types her email, and then a password that is not right. The page tells her, invalid email or password. She tries again, and again, and a fourth time. Each time she sees the same plain message, and each miss is counted.

On the fifth wrong password, the page changes. Imani is taken to a screen that says, too many sign-in attempts. It explains that the account is temporarily locked, and that she can try again in about fifteen minutes, or reset her password.

The lock protects Imani. Someone who was only guessing her password has now been stopped. And the lockout is recorded, so the program can see it in its security monitoring.

[pause 2s]

## Two ways back

Imani does not have to sit and wait with no options. The locked screen offers two buttons.

The first way back is the one that says, reset password. It opens the page where she enters her email and asks for a reset link. That page works even while the account is locked.

The second way back is the one that says, contact your mentor. It takes her to the contact page on the Waypoint public site. There she can write to the program, and a person can help her.

Both paths stay open for as long as the lock lasts. Nobody is shut out of getting help.

[pause 2s]

## Trying again

If Imani would rather wait, that works too. When the lock time has passed, she can sign in again. No one has to unlock the account for her, and she does not need to ask for permission. The default lock is fifteen minutes, and the program can configure that value.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Five wrong passwords lock the account. The locked screen says so, and gives the lock time. Resetting the password and contacting the mentor both stay available during the lock. When the time is up, the person can sign in again on their own. Every lockout is logged for security monitoring.
