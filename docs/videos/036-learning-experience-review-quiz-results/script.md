# 036 · Reviewing a quiz result

## Welcome

After a youth submits a quiz, the next question is always the same. How did I do? This video shows what the youth sees on the quiz result page, and why the score may not appear right away.

[pause 2s]

## Waiting for approval

Amara has just submitted her module quiz. The result page says, waiting for mentor approval. Your answers are submitted and locked. Your score will appear after review.

No number is shown yet. For a quiz that needs a mentor to approve it, Waypoint never shows the youth a score that has not been approved. Her answers are locked, so nothing she does now can change them.

[pause 2s]

## The approved result

After her mentor approves the attempt, Amara reloads the page. Now she sees the approved score, a hundred percent, and the word passed, next to the pass mark of seventy percent.

Her attempt earned a badge, and the badge appears on the page with its name and what it was for. Pass the foundations module quiz. A badge is awarded once, and the page shows it as earned.

[pause 2s]

## Question review

Below the score is the question review. Each question is listed with a label, correct or not. Under it are the answers, with her answer and the correct answer marked. Where she picked a wrong answer, the review marks her wrong selection. How much detail shows depends on the feedback mode that staff chose for the quiz.

[pause 2s]

## Retaking

At the bottom is a retake quiz button. It is offered when the quiz allows a retake and she still has attempts left. Pressing it starts a new attempt.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

A quiz that needs approval shows no score until it is approved. The result shows the score, passed or not against the pass mark, and any badge earned, once. The question review depends on the feedback mode. Retake appears only when attempts remain.
