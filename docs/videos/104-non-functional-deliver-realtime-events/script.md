# 104 · Live notifications and messages

## Welcome

When a mentor sends a message, a young person should not have to refresh the page to see it. Waypoint delivers notifications and messages as they happen, and only to the people who are meant to see them. This video shows what that looks like, and what keeps it safe.

[pause 2s]

## A notification arrives

Amara Bell opens her notifications page. It says, you're all caught up, and next to the title there is a small badge that reads, live updates on. That badge tells her the page is connected and listening.

Now Grace Adeyemi, her mentor, sends her a message. Amara does not touch anything. A new item appears on her page. It says, new message, you have a new message, with a badge marked new and a link to open it. Nobody reloaded the page. The notification simply arrived.

[pause 2s]

## A message arrives

The same thing happens in the conversation itself. Amara opens her messages with Grace. The badge says live updates on again. Grace sends another message, and it appears in the thread by itself, below the earlier ones. A short status line at the top says, new message from your mentor. It is also announced to people who use a screen reader, so they find out as well.

[pause 2s]

## Safe and steady

Two things keep this safe. First, to connect at all, the page must present a valid sign-in token. Waypoint then places it only in the groups that person is allowed to be in. Their own, their cohort, or their role. So a message for Amara goes to Amara, and does not go to anyone else. A notification also carries nothing the person is not allowed to see.

Second, connections drop sometimes. When the page reconnects, it reloads the latest items, and an item it already has is not added a second time. So nothing is missed, and nothing appears twice.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Notifications and messages show up as they happen, with a live updates badge to say the page is listening. They go only to the people they are meant for. After a dropped connection, the page catches up by itself, without duplicates.
