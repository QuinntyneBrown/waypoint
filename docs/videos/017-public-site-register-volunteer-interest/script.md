# 017 · Offering to volunteer

## Welcome

Waypoint depends on volunteers. People who give their time to young leaders. Because they will work with young people, the program wants to be clear with them from the very first step. This short video shows the public volunteer page, and how it records one important acknowledgement.

[pause 2s]

## The get involved page

Sam Whitfield wants to help. Sam opens the public site and chooses Get involved in the top menu. The page is titled, help raise up the next generation. Right at the top it says that, because volunteers work with youth, every volunteer completes a screening and reference check before starting.

Two cards sit beside each other. One is for people who would rather give monthly. The other is for people who have a question first, and it leads to the contact page. Below them is the volunteer form.

[pause 2s]

## The volunteer form

The form asks for a name and an email address, and both are required. There is also an optional phone number, and a box that asks, how would you like to help? Sam writes that tutoring in the coding track on weekday evenings would suit.

Last of all is a checkbox. It says that volunteers working with youth must complete a screening and reference check before starting.

[pause 2s]

## The acknowledgement

Suppose Sam presses sign up to volunteer without ticking the box. Waypoint stops there. A message appears under the box. Please acknowledge the screening and reference check. Nothing is sent.

Sam ticks the box, and presses the button again. The page now says, thanks for volunteering. It explains that the team will reach out about next steps, including the screening and reference check every youth volunteer completes before starting.

This does not make Sam a volunteer. It records Sam's interest, along with the fact that Sam knows about the check. The screening itself comes afterwards.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

The volunteer page is open to everyone. The screening and reference check is explained at the top of the page and on the form. Sign up is blocked until the visitor acknowledges it. A thank you page tells the volunteer what happens next.
