# 085 · Choosing which notifications you get

## Welcome

Waypoint can tell people when something happens, such as work being graded or a message arriving. Not everyone wants every kind of message, and not everyone wants email. This video shows where each person chooses.

[pause 2s]

## The notifications page

Meet Grace, a mentor. She opens her settings and chooses the notifications tab. The page says, choose how you would like to hear from Waypoint. Changes save automatically.

Below that is a list of kinds of events. Lesson reminders. Mentor messages. Session reminders. Work graded. Badges earned. At-risk alerts. Approval needed. And announcements. Each one has a short line that says what it is. For example, at-risk alerts says, when an assigned youth needs follow-up.

Every event has two boxes. One is labelled in-app. The other is labelled email. In-app means the message shows inside Waypoint. Email is the optional extra channel, for people who also want a message in their inbox.

[pause 2s]

## Changing a choice

Grace scrolls down to announcements, which are program-wide news and updates. For her, the in-app box starts out empty, so announcements are off. She decides she wants them, and ticks the in-app box.

There is no save button to hunt for. The change is stored the moment she ticks the box, and a short message confirms it. Notification preferences saved.

[pause 2s]

## It sticks, and it is respected

Grace reloads the page. The announcements box is still ticked. Her choice lives with her account.

The requirement behind this page is clear. When a person has turned off a channel for an event, no notification is sent to them on that channel. Turning a box off is a real choice. It is not only a setting on a page.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Notification settings list each kind of event, with a separate in-app box and email box. A change saves the moment it is made, and a message confirms it. The choice survives a reload. And when a channel is off for an event, Waypoint does not send on that channel.
