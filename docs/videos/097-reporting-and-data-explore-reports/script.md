# 097 · Exploring reports

## Welcome

Every program needs to answer a simple question: how are we doing? In Waypoint, the answer starts at the reports hub. This short video shows how program staff explore reports, and how the numbers always match the filters they choose.

[pause 2s]

## Filtering the hub

Meet Pat Morgan, a program administrator. Pat opens the reports hub from the admin menu. At the top sits a card called report filters, and under it a line that says every overview and staff metric uses this exact scope.

There are five filters. Pat can set a date from and a date to, and then pick a cohort, a track, and a mentor. Say Pat chooses one cohort, its track, and its lead mentor, Grace Adeyemi, and presses apply report filters.

Right away the hub recomputes. Under the filters, Waypoint shows when the report was updated, and how many youth records it covers. Here it says one youth record. Below that are six figures: active youth, average completion, attendance, retention, cohorts, and active mentors. Every figure follows the filters Pat just chose. If Pat sets the end date before the start date, the page says so, and waits for a valid range.

[pause 2s]

## Four views

The hub has four views. Overview shows the figures and charts for the whole scope. Youth report opens one young person. Cohort report opens one cohort. Staff activity shows what each mentor is doing. The next videos look at the youth, cohort, and staff views one by one. There are also buttons to export the current report, which has a video of its own.

Reports respect who is asking. A mentor sees only the reports for their own youth, so a report never reveals more than its reader is allowed to see.

[pause 2s]

## When there is nothing to show

Sometimes a filter matches nobody. Pat picks a brand new cohort that has no youth yet, and applies the filters. The page does not show a blank grid of zeros. It shows a clear message. No report data for these filters. Change the date or program filters and try again.

And if the report cannot load, Pat does not see a half-drawn page. Waypoint shows a plain error, something went wrong, with a try again button. One press, and the report loads again.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

The reports hub has five filters: dates, cohort, track, and mentor. Every figure follows them, with the time it was updated and the number of youth records. An empty scope gets a clear message, and a failure gets a try again button. Mentors only ever see their own youth.
