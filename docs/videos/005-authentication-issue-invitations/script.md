# 005 · Sending invitations

## Welcome

Waypoint is invitation only, so every account starts with someone on staff sending an invitation. This video shows how Pat Morgan, a program administrator, invites a group of new youth, how a mentor invites youth of their own, and who is not allowed to send invitations at all.

[pause 2s]

## The invitation form

Pat opens the invitations page. On the left is a card called send invitations. Pat chooses a role, in this case youth. Pat can also choose a cohort and a mentor, and both are optional. If Pat leaves them empty, they can be assigned later. A note on the card says that links expire after fourteen days.

Below that is one box for email addresses. It takes one address per line. Pat types three addresses, one for each new youth, and presses send invitation.

[pause 2s]

## What happens next

Waypoint reports the result in plain words. It says, three invitations sent. One invitation is created for each address, all with the same role, cohort and mentor. Each person receives an email with their own link.

On the right, Pat can see every invitation. Each row shows the email address, the role and context, a status, and when it expires. These three show the status pending, which means nobody has accepted yet. Each pending row also has resend and revoke buttons, which the next videos cover.

[pause 2s]

## Mentors and everyone else

Grace Adeyemi is a mentor. When she opens the same page, it looks a little different. A note tells her that invitations she sends create youth accounts assigned to her. She can only invite youth, and there is no role to pick.

A youth, though, cannot send invitations at all. If Amara Bell tried to open the administrator's invitations page, she would see a page that says, you don't have access to that. Waypoint decides this on the server, so hiding a button is never the only protection.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Staff invite by email and role, with an optional cohort and mentor. One box can take many addresses, and Waypoint reports how many were sent. Every new invitation starts as pending, and the page says links expire after fourteen days. Mentors can invite youth only, and other roles cannot invite at all.
