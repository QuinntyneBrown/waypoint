# 27 · First-run onboarding

## Welcome

The first time a young person arrives in Waypoint, they see a short guided setup. It has three steps, none of them hard, and all of it can be skipped. This video follows Amara Bell through it, right after she has set her password.

[pause 2s]

## Step one, your profile

The first step is called, tell us about you. It says, step one of three. Her display name is already filled in from the invitation, and a star marks it as the one required answer. Below it is a box for pronouns, and Amara types hers.

Then there is a place for a profile photo. It is optional. The page says it can be a J P G or P N G, up to five megabytes, and that it is stored privately. Amara leaves the photo empty and presses next.

[pause 2s]

## Step two, your cohort

Step two is called, your cohort. Amara does not choose anything here. Her cohort and mentor were set by the program team when they sent her invitation, so the page simply confirms it. It says, you are placed in a cohort, and your mentor will guide your journey.

[pause 2s]

## When there is no cohort yet

Not everyone is placed on day one. For a youth who has not been placed, this step says so honestly. Cohorts are assigned by your program team, you do not pick one. You are not placed in a cohort yet, your mentor will add you soon, and program content unlocks once you are placed.

[pause 2s]

## Step three, notifications

The last step is notifications. Waypoint starts with sensible defaults. Lesson reminders and mentor messages are on, along with most of the others. Announcements are off. Amara can flip any of these switches now, and the page reminds her that she can change them anytime in settings. She presses finish.

[pause 2s]

## Landing at home

Amara arrives on her youth home page, which welcomes her by name, and shows her next step in the curriculum. Her setup is done.

Suppose she had pressed skip for now, at any step. That works too. She would land on the same home page, and nobody is held back from their program by a form.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Onboarding has three steps: profile, cohort, and notifications. Only the display name is required. Cohort and mentor are set by staff, and a youth who is not placed yet is told so plainly. Announcements start off. Skip for now leads to the home page.
