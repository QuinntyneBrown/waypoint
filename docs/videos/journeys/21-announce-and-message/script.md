# 21 · Announce and message

## Welcome

Some news is for a whole group. Some words are for one person. Waypoint has a place for each. This video follows Pat Morgan, a program administrator, as she publishes an announcement, and Grace Adeyemi, a mentor, as she talks with Amara Bell in a private thread, and then sends a reminder to her whole group.

[pause 2s]

## Composing an announcement

Pat opens the announcement page, and fills in the compose form. A title, and a message. Then she chooses who should get it. The audience can be the whole organization, or one cohort. The recipients can be youth, youth and guardians together, guardians, mentors, or everyone. Pat picks one cohort, and youth only.

At the bottom is the delivery choice. Send now delivers it straight away. Schedule for later asks for a date and a time, and Waypoint sends it then. Pat chooses send now.

[pause 2s]

## What recipients see

The announcement appears in Pat's list with a sent label, the cohort and the audience beside it. It shows zero of one read, and a read rate of zero percent.

Amara opens her announcements page. The new one is marked new. Only announcements meant for her audience and role appear there. When she opens or marks them read, the new label goes away, and Pat's count goes up. That tells Pat whether the message actually reached people.

[pause 2s]

## A private thread

Announcements are for groups. Messages are private. Amara opens her messages page. It says, your private conversation with Grace Adeyemi, and a small badge says, live updates on. That means new messages appear without a refresh. She writes, I finished today's lesson, and sends it.

On her side, Grace has an inbox with one conversation for each youth, and a search box. She opens Amara's conversation, reads the message, and replies, great work, see you at the next session. Amara's page updates by itself, with a short notice that a new message has arrived from her mentor.

Messages can only be read by the two people in the thread, and a person can only message people their role allows.

[pause 2s]

## Broadcast to my group

Sometimes Grace has the same news for everyone. Beside the inbox is a button, broadcast to my group. She writes her reminder, bring your journal on Monday, and presses review. Before anything is sent, Waypoint asks her to confirm, and says exactly how many people will receive it. Here, one private message to one youth.

When Grace confirms, each youth receives the reminder as an individual personal message, in their own thread with her. Youth never see who else received it. Amara's thread now shows the reminder, right below the earlier conversation.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Announcements go to a chosen audience and role, sent now or scheduled, and staff can follow how many have read them. Messages are private between a youth and their mentor, and arrive live. A broadcast is sent as one personal message per youth, after a review step that shows the recipient count.
