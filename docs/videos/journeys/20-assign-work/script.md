# 20 · Assign work

## Welcome

A mentor does not only review work. A mentor also decides what a young person should do next. This video follows Grace Adeyemi as she assigns a lesson to Amara Bell, and then shows what Amara sees.

[pause 2s]

## Choosing what to assign

Grace opens Assign work. The page says she can add a published lesson, module, or project to individual journeys or a whole cohort. The first question is, what are you assigning? She picks Lesson, and then chooses the lesson from the list. A type and an item are both required.

A summary panel on the right follows along. It shows the item, how many youth are selected, and the due date.

[pause 2s]

## Choosing who and when

Next comes, who is this for. Grace can choose selected youth, or the whole cohort. She picks Amara. Waypoint shows a small tag by a name when a youth is at risk, so a mentor can notice who might need extra support.

Then Grace sets a due date, and a reminder. She chooses to be reminded two days before it is due. Last is an optional message to the youth. Grace writes, please complete this before our next session. A note under the box explains that each recipient receives the message in their mentor message thread.

[pause 2s]

## Review and confirm

Grace presses review and assign. Nothing is created yet. A confirmation window asks her to check her choices. It names the item and the due date, says the work will be added to one youth journey, names who will receive it, says the message goes out right away, and says that a due reminder will be scheduled for each person.

[pause 2s]

## Done

Only when Grace presses, assign to one youth, does Waypoint create the assignment. One record is made for each recipient. The page reports it. Assigned Living the Word to one youth, so the mentor knows it worked.

[pause 2s]

## What Amara sees

Now Amara signs in. On her home page, under assigned work from her mentor, the lesson appears. It shows the title, the label assigned, the due date, and Grace's message. A button, open, takes her straight to it. If she wants to ask a question, there is also a button to message Grace.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

A type and an item are required. The recipients are selected youth or a whole cohort. A due date, a reminder and a message are all available. Nothing happens until the mentor confirms. After that, each recipient finds the work on their home page, and the count is reported to the mentor.
