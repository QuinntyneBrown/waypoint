# 04 · Accept invitation

## Welcome

In Waypoint, nobody signs themselves up. People join because someone invited them. This video follows one invitation from the moment staff send it, to the first screen inside Waypoint, and shows what happens when a link can no longer be used.

[pause 2s]

## Sending the invitation

Pat Morgan, a program administrator, opens Invitations. The page says, invite people by role and track every link. Pat picks the role, youth. A cohort and a mentor can be chosen too, but both are optional. Then Pat types the email addresses, one per line. A note says that links expire after fourteen days.

Pat presses send invitation, and the page says the invitations were sent. They appear in a list, marked pending, with a button to resend and a button to revoke.

[pause 2s]

## Opening the link

Amara Bell receives an email with a personal link. She opens it and sees a page that says, join Waypoint as youth. Under the heading it shows the invitation is for her address, but with part of it hidden, so a stranger who finds the link learns very little.

[pause 2s]

## Setting up the account

Amara fills in three things. First, the display name she wants people to see. Second, a password, which she types twice. There is an eye button if she wants to check what she typed. Third, she ticks a box to say she agrees to follow the community guidelines. Without that tick, she cannot join.

When she presses join Waypoint, her account is created with the role from the invitation. Then Amara goes straight to the first time setup, which is the subject of another video.

[pause 2s]

## Links that no longer work

An invitation link works once. If Amara opens the same link again, the page says, this invite has already been used, and tells her to sign in with the password she created.

A link also stops working if it expires, or if staff revoke it. Pat can revoke any pending invitation, and the old link stops straight away. The page then says, this invite is no longer active, and offers a button, request a new invite. Staff are not stuck either, because they can resend an invitation to give a fresh link.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

An invitation is a personal link, sent by staff, with a role and optionally a cohort and a mentor. To accept it, you choose a display name, a password, and agree to the community guidelines. The link works once. Expired or revoked links do not work, and the page offers a way to ask for a new invite.
