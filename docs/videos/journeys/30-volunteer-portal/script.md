# 30 · Volunteer portal

## Welcome

Volunteers give their time to help run Waypoint sessions, and they need to know where to be and what to do. This video follows Sam Whitfield, a volunteer, through a working day: finding today's session, taking attendance, and looking ahead at the schedule. It also shows what a volunteer is never shown.

[pause 2s]

## Today's session

Sam signs in. Waypoint takes volunteers straight to their own home page, the same way a mentor or a youth goes to theirs. The page is headed today's session, and a line underneath says, your assignment for today, at a glance.

One card shows the session. It has the title, the day and the start time, and how long it lasts, ninety minutes. Under that is the location, the main hall, and a head count. Three on the roster. It is a number and nothing else. The card has no names and nothing about the young people.

At the bottom is one button, take attendance.

[pause 2s]

## Taking attendance

Sam presses the button and the roster opens. The page says that only names and present or absent attendance are shown. There are three young people, Amara, David, and Imani, and beside each name a present button and an absent button. There is no progress, no notes, and no wellbeing flag. A volunteer sees a name and nothing more.

Three small tiles keep a running count of how many are marked, present, and absent. Most sessions have mostly present youth, so Sam presses all present, then presses absent beside David. The tiles now read three of three marked, two present, one absent.

Sam presses save attendance, and a message says, attendance saved. If the signal drops during a session, the change is kept and sent when the connection returns, so no marks are lost.

[pause 2s]

## Looking ahead

Sam opens the schedule from the volunteer menu. The page is headed your schedule, and lists every session Sam is assigned to help with, nearest first. Each card shows the title, the day and time, the length, and the location. A badge at the right gives the status. The session Sam just finished now shows that attendance was taken, and the later ones say upcoming.

Only Sam's own sessions appear. A session that belongs to another volunteer does not, and neither does any youth information. If a volunteer asks for anything outside that scope, Waypoint refuses, because every request is checked on the server, not only on the screen.

[pause 2s]

## A day with nothing assigned

Now suppose Sam signs in on a day with no assignment. The home page does not show an error or a blank screen. It says, no session today, explains that Sam has no assigned session today, and offers a button, view schedule. A brand new volunteer sees a similar calm message on the schedule, until staff assign a session.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Volunteers land on today's session, with one card and a head count, never names. Attendance shows names and a present or absent choice, and nothing else about anyone. The schedule lists only the volunteer's own sessions. Empty days get a clear message instead of an error.
