# 23 · The guardian portal

## Welcome

A guardian wants to know how their young person is getting on, without reading private notes or lesson content. This video follows Ruth Clarke through the guardian portal, from her home page, to progress and schedule, to the consent record she has signed.

[pause 2s]

## Home at a glance

Ruth Clarke signs in as a guardian, and she lands on a page called Your youth at a glance. The name of her young person, Imani Clarke, sits underneath. Three tiles show overall progress, the attendance rate, and how many badges Imani has earned.

Below them, a card called What's next shows the next session. A button, View full progress, takes Ruth deeper.

[pause 2s]

## Progress and attendance

The progress page is read only. At the top is a list of modules. Each one shows its status and how far through it Imani is, as a percentage.

Next comes the attendance history. Every session is listed with its date and a status. Here, Imani was present at one session and absent at another.

Further down are approved quiz outcomes, leadership competencies assessed by a mentor, and badges. Badges Imani has earned are listed with a star. Badges still to unlock are listed with an open circle, so Ruth can see what Imani is working towards.

[pause 2s]

## Schedule and shared updates

The upcoming schedule lists the next sessions with their dates and times. Here it shows a family showcase.

Last, a card called Shared mentor updates shows check-in notes, but only the ones the mentor chose to share. Ruth reads that Imani was engaged and contributing, and what the next steps are. A check-in marked private never appears here.

There is no button to edit anything. No lesson content, no quiz answers, and no private reflections are shown. A guardian only ever sees their own linked young person.

[pause 2s]

## Consent

Ruth opens the Consent page from the menu. The Participation consent card shows an Active badge, who signed and how they are related to Imani, the date, the method, how long the consent is valid, and a reference number. She can email herself a copy, or withdraw the consent after a confirmation. The same page keeps her contact details and her media choices.

Ruth can also read news and announcements sent to guardians or to her child's cohort, from the News page.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Guardians get a read only view of progress, attendance, badges, and the schedule. Only approved quiz outcomes and shared mentor updates are shown. Nothing can be edited, and nothing private is visible. Consent and contact details live on one page, and they belong to that guardian alone.
