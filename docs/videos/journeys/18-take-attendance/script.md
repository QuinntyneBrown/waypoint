# 18 · Take attendance

## Welcome

Attendance is one of the most important records a youth program keeps. This video follows Grace Adeyemi, a mentor, as she takes attendance for a session, and then takes it again when the room has no internet.

[pause 2s]

## The roster

From the calendar, Grace presses Take attendance on the session. The page shows the session title, the day, the time and the place. Below it is the roster, with one row for each youth. Here there is one youth, Amara Bell.

Across the top is a running tally. It says how many youth are marked out of how many, and then counts present, late, excused and absent. Right now, nobody is marked.

[pause 2s]

## Marking

Each row has four buttons. Present, late, excused and absent. Amara arrived a little behind the others, so Grace presses Late. The tally updates straight away, and now says one of one marked, and one late.

Next to the buttons is a staff note. Grace writes, transit delay, and Amara joined ready to participate. For a big group there is a faster way. Press All present, and everyone is marked present. Clear puts everything back to unmarked.

[pause 2s]

## Saving

Grace presses Save attendance, and the page says, attendance saved. From that moment, the session has an attendance record. It feeds the attendance rates that staff review later, and the streak rules for badges.

If the same session is saved again, Waypoint checks that nobody else changed it in the meantime, so one person's edit cannot quietly overwrite another's.

[pause 2s]

## When the connection drops

Sessions do not always happen where the signal is good. Suppose Grace's connection drops. A dark banner appears across the top. It says, you're offline, changes will save when you reconnect.

Grace keeps working. She presses All present, and adds a note. Then she presses Save attendance. Waypoint cannot reach the server, so it keeps the marks on her device. The page says, saved offline, one attendance update waiting to sync, and a small badge says one pending sync.

When the connection returns, Grace does not have to do anything. Waypoint uploads the waiting update by itself, and the page says, all attendance changes synced. Each update carries a unique key, so if it is sent twice, no duplicate record is created.

If someone else changed the session while Grace was offline, Waypoint does not guess and does not overwrite anything silently. It shows that her changes conflict with a newer server edit, and lets her choose which to keep.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Each youth is marked present, late, excused or absent, with a running tally and an optional note. Saving makes the record count toward attendance rates and badge streaks. With no connection, marks are saved on the device and sync by themselves, without duplicates. A conflict is shown clearly, and nothing is lost silently.
