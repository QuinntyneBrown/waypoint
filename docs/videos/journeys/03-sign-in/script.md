# 03 · Sign in

## Welcome

Everything in Waypoint starts at one door. This video follows two people through it, Amara and Grace, and then shows what happens when someone gets the password wrong too many times.

[pause 2s]

## Opening the door

Meet Amara Bell. She is a youth in the program, and today she opens Waypoint. She sees a simple page. It says, welcome back, and, sign in to continue your journey. There are two boxes, email and password, and a button that says sign in. Under the password, a small link says, forgot password.

Amara is in a hurry, and she mistypes her password. The page does not tell her which part was wrong. It says, invalid email or password. That is the same message anyone would see for an email Waypoint has never met, so nobody can use this page to find out who is in the program.

She types it again, correctly, and presses sign in.

[pause 2s]

## Landing at home

Waypoint takes Amara straight to her own home page. It greets her by name, and the menu on the side shows what a youth needs: her journey, her progress, her curriculum, her badges, her schedule, and her messages. Her account has not been placed in a cohort yet, so the page says so, and offers a button to message her mentor.

Now meet Grace Adeyemi, a mentor. She uses the very same page and the same kind of sign-in. But Waypoint sends her somewhere different. Grace lands on the mentor dashboard, which says, welcome back, and shows her youth, her pending reviews, and her at-risk count. Each person goes to the place made for their role, and sees only what that role is allowed to see.

[pause 2s]

## Too many guesses

Now picture Imani Clarke, who has forgotten her password and keeps trying. Waypoint counts every wrong attempt. After the fifth one, the account is locked, and Imani is taken to a page that says, too many sign-in attempts.

The page explains that the account is temporarily locked, and that she can try again in about fifteen minutes. She is not stuck. Two buttons stay open the whole time. One says reset password, and the other says, contact your mentor. So a lock stops someone guessing, without leaving the real owner of the account with nowhere to go.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

One page, one email, one password. A wrong guess reveals nothing about who has an account. A correct sign-in sends each person to the home made for their role. Five wrong passwords lock the account for a while, and the person can still ask for a reset or contact their mentor.
