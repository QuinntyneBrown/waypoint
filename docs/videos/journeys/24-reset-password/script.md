# 24 · Reset password

## Welcome

Everyone forgets a password sometimes. This video follows Amara from the moment she is stuck to the moment she is back in, and shows why the whole path is safe, even for someone who types the wrong email.

[pause 2s]

## Asking for a link

Amara Bell opens the sign-in page, and cannot remember her password. Under the password box there is a link that says, forgot password. She chooses it.

A page titled, reset your password, asks for one thing, her email. She types it and presses, send reset link.

The next page says, check your email. It says that if an account exists for her address, a link has been emailed, and that the link expires in one hour. Her address is shown only in part, with the first letter and the domain, so someone looking over her shoulder learns very little. If the email does not arrive, she can press resend link.

Here is the careful part. If someone types an address Waypoint has never seen, they get exactly the same page and exactly the same words. The reset page cannot be used to find out who is in the program.

[pause 2s]

## Choosing a new password

Amara opens the link in her email. The page says, set a new password. She types one, and types it again to confirm. A hint under the first box gives the rule: at least eight characters, including a letter and a number. When the password follows the rule and both boxes match, the reset password button is ready, and she presses it.

[pause 2s]

## Back in

Amara sees a page that says, your password was updated. It adds that, for her security, her other sessions were signed out. So if anyone else was signed in as Amara, they are out now.

She presses sign in, uses her new password, and lands on her home page, as she always does.

[pause 2s]

## When the link does not work

A reset link works once, and for one hour. If Amara opens the same link a second time, or opens an old one, the page says, this link is invalid or expired. It explains that links can only be used once and expire after an hour, and offers two buttons, request a new link, and back to sign in. Nothing else has changed, and she can simply start again.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Anyone can ask for a reset, and the answer never reveals who has an account. The address is shown only in part. Each link works once and expires after one hour. A new password must follow the rule, and both boxes must match. After a reset, other sessions are signed out.
