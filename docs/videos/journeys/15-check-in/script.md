# 15 · Check in

## Welcome

Mentoring happens in conversations. A phone call, a visit, a quick chat after a session. Waypoint gives a mentor one place to write each conversation down, and to decide who gets to see it. This video follows Grace Adeyemi, a mentor, as she records a check-in with Amara Bell.

[pause 2s]

## Starting from the record

Grace opens Amara's record, and chooses the Check-ins tab. It lists the conversations Grace has already had with her. Each line gives the type, whether it is private, and the date. Here there is one earlier one-to-one, marked private. A button opens the full journal.

[pause 2s]

## The check-in journal

Grace presses the button, and the Check-ins page opens with Amara already chosen. On the left is a form to log a new check-in. On the right is the history for that youth, with filters for the youth and for the type of conversation. Each entry shows its type, its date, and a label that says whether it is a private mentor note or shared. The older entry is private. Only Grace, and staff who are allowed to, can read it.

[pause 2s]

## Writing it down

A check-in needs three things. The youth, the date, and the type of conversation. Grace picks a call. Then she adds the rest. How Amara seemed, which Waypoint calls engagement or mood. The goals they discussed. Any prayer requests. Action items. And a date for the next follow-up if she wants one.

[pause 2s]

## Who can see it

Near the bottom, a switch says, share this check-in with the youth and guardian. It starts off. A check-in is a private mentor note unless Grace chooses otherwise.

Prayer requests get extra care. They stay with the mentor, even when other fields are shared. The only way they are shared is if Grace switches on sharing herself. When she does, Waypoint says so in plain words. All entered fields, including prayer requests, will be visible to the youth and their linked guardian.

There is one more box. Guardian was informed. Grace has already spoken with Amara's guardian, so she ticks it, and that is recorded on the check-in.

[pause 2s]

## Saved

Grace presses Save check-in, and a message confirms it. The new entry appears at the top of the history. It carries the label Shared, and the note that the guardian was informed. A shared check-in also shows on the youth's progress page.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Every check-in needs a youth, a date, and a type. Sharing is off by default, so a check-in is private unless the mentor decides otherwise. Prayer requests are mentor-only unless sharing is switched on. The history can be filtered by youth and by type.
