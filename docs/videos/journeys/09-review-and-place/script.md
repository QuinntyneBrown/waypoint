# 09 · Review and place

## Welcome

Applications from the public site all land in one place, a queue for the program administrator. This video follows Pat Morgan, a program administrator, through two applications. One is from Imani Clarke, who is fifteen and has no guardian consent yet. The other is from Amara Bell, who is eighteen and ready to be placed.

[pause 2s]

## The queue

Pat opens the applications page. Its subtitle says, review consent, place applicants, and keep every decision traceable. Below it is a table with one row for each application. Each row shows the applicant, their age, their neighbourhood, the track they are interested in, a status, and the date they applied.

Applicants under eighteen carry a small badge that says minor. The status tells Pat where each one stands. An application can be new, in review, needs consent, approved, or declined. Imani's says needs consent. Amara's says new.

[pause 2s]

## A minor without consent

Pat presses review on Imani's row, and a review window opens. A yellow notice says, guardian participation consent required. It explains that this minor cannot be approved into active participation until a valid consent record is linked.

Below the notice is everything the applicant submitted, including the guardian's name and contact details. And look at the approve button. It is still available. Approving places Imani in a cohort, but she cannot take part until a guardian's consent is linked. Pat does not have to remember the rule. Waypoint enforces it.

[pause 2s]

## Declining softly

Pat decides there is no place for Imani this term, and types a note, no place is available this term. Pat presses decline, and Waypoint asks one more question. Decline this applicant without creating an account? It adds that the optional note remains on the application record.

Pat confirms. The status changes to declined. Nothing is deleted, and no account is ever created. That is why the program calls it a soft decline.

[pause 2s]

## Placing Amara

Now for Amara. Pat opens her review, and sets her interest track. Then Pat chooses a cohort and a mentor. Both are required, and the mentor list is limited to the mentors assigned to that cohort.

Pat adds a note, excellent fit for this cohort, and presses approve. The confirmation names the cohort and the mentor. It asks, enrol this applicant in this cohort with this mentor? And it warns that the applicant contact will receive an approval email.

Pat confirms, and the page says Amara was approved and enrolled. Behind that message, Waypoint creates her youth account and enrolls her in the cohort under the mentor Pat chose. Every approval and every decline is written to the audit log.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Every application lands in one queue, and minors are clearly marked. A minor without guardian consent can be approved, but cannot take part until that consent is linked. A decline is soft. An approval needs a cohort and a mentor, and it creates the account and enrolls the youth. Only program administrators decide, and every decision is audited.
