# 07 · Manage cohorts

## Welcome

A cohort is a group of young people who move through the program together, and every young person works alongside a mentor. This video follows Pat Morgan, a program administrator, as Pat creates a cohort, adds Amara Bell to it, and makes sure the mentors are not overloaded.

[pause 2s]

## Creating a cohort

Pat opens Cohorts from the menu and presses new cohort. A window called create cohort asks for a cohort name, a curriculum track, and a lead mentor. Those three are required. Pat also enters a start date, an end date, and a meeting location. Here the cohort is the Fall cohort, and it meets at the community hall.

If something is missing, the form says what to fix. And the end date must come after the start date.

Pat presses create cohort, and a card appears. It shows the track, the lead mentor, the number of members, the location, and the dates.

[pause 2s]

## Adding youth

Pat opens the new cohort. It has no members yet, and the page says so. In the panel called add members, Pat chooses Amara Bell from the list of youth and presses add youth.

Waypoint confirms that the youth was added to the cohort and enrolled in its track. Amara now appears in the member list, and the member count goes up to one. If the young person is not in Waypoint yet, an invite a youth button leads to the invitations page instead.

[pause 2s]

## Pairing with a mentor

Next, Pat opens Mentor assignments. The first panel is called pair a youth. Pat picks a young person, then picks a mentor. Every mentor in the list shows how many youth they already carry. Pat presses assign mentor.

Waypoint saves the pairing, and the change is visible on the mentor's roster straight away. When Grace Adeyemi signs in, the young person is already on her list.

[pause 2s]

## Watching the load

Further down, every active mentor has a card. It shows the mentor's name, how many youth they carry, their check-ins, and their reviews. A badge compares the load with a target band of four to eight active youth. After Pat's pairing, Grace's card shows one youth, and the badge says under target. This is how Pat spots a mentor who is stretched, or one who has room.

The second panel is the cohort mentor team. Pat chooses a cohort and sets one lead mentor, then ticks any supporting mentors. The roles belong to the cohort, so a mentor can lead one cohort and support another.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

A cohort needs a name, a track, and a lead mentor. Youth are added from the cohort page, and the member count follows. A pairing takes effect at once and shows on the mentor's roster. Each mentor's card shows their load against a target band.
