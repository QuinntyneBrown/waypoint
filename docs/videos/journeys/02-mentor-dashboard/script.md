# 02 · Mentor dashboard

## Welcome

Every morning, a mentor needs to know three things. How is my group doing, what is waiting for me, and who needs attention first. This video follows Grace Adeyemi, a mentor, from the moment she signs in to the moment she opens the record of one young person. That young person is David Osei, who is falling behind.

Waypoint shows Grace only her own youth. That rule holds on every screen in this journey.

[pause 2s]

## The dashboard

Grace signs in, and lands on her dashboard. At the top are four numbers. My youth, pending reviews, average progress, and at risk. Under pending reviews, Waypoint also shows how many are overdue, in red, so late work stands out. Here Grace has one youth, two reviews waiting, and one of them overdue.

Every number is worked out only over the youth assigned to Grace. A youth who belongs to another mentor is never counted, and never shown.

Below the numbers is a short list of her youth, with each one's progress. David carries an At risk tag. Further down is a preview of the reviews queue. Each item names the youth and the kind of work, and says whether it is due or overdue, with a button that goes straight to grading.

[pause 2s]

## The roster

Grace presses View all youth. The My youth page lists everyone assigned to her. For each youth she sees the cohort, progress, when they were last active, and their status. A search box finds a name, and four filters narrow the list. All, On track, At risk, and New.

Grace presses At risk, and the list narrows to David, whose status reads At risk.

If a search matches nobody, the list does not just go blank. A message says so, and a button clears the filters. A new mentor with nobody assigned yet sees zeros and a message, not an error.

[pause 2s]

## David's record

Grace opens David. His name is at the top, with his cohort, and a note says this is a mentor-only coaching record. Right below it is a red banner. It says David is at risk, gives the reason, follow up after two missed sessions, and says who flagged him, and when. So before she reads anything else, Grace knows why he needs attention.

The record is split into tabs. Profile, Progress, Attendance, Check-ins, Submissions, and Competencies. Profile shows his email and cohort, beside the guardian contact. Guardian contact is sensitive, so only the assigned mentor and program staff can see it.

Grace opens the Attendance tab. David was present at a Leadership Lab, and absent from a Community Night. If a mentor tries to open a youth who is not assigned to them, Waypoint refuses.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

The dashboard shows four counts for the mentor's own youth only, and calls out overdue reviews. The roster can be searched, and filtered by All, On track, At risk, and New. A youth's record opens with an at-risk banner that says why. Every tab is one step from the next coaching action. A mentor never sees a youth who is not assigned to them.
