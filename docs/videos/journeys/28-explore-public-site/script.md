# 28 · Explore the public site

## Welcome

Waypoint has a public front door. Anyone can open it without an account, learn what the program offers, and decide how to take part. This video follows Sam Whitfield, a visitor who is curious about the program and wants to help.

[pause 2s]

## The home page

Sam opens the public site and lands on the home page. No sign in page is in the way. A headline says, raising up young leaders who love Jesus. Two buttons sit under it, apply now and explore the programs.

Further down is a four step journey, from apply to graduate, and a row of numbers. They count the program's tracks, cohorts, modules, and neighbourhoods. They never count individual young people, so the page shows the size of the program without sharing anything private.

[pause 2s]

## Programs and cohorts

Sam chooses Programs in the top menu. The page shows one card for each published track. In this example there are two, Bible and Leadership, and Coding for Kingdom. Each card lists its modules in order, with the outcome badge each module leads to. Only published content appears.

Next, Sam opens About. It lists the current cohorts. Each card shows the cohort's name, its neighbourhood, its dates, its lead mentor, and whether it is upcoming or active. No young person's name or details appear here.

[pause 2s]

## Getting involved

Sam wants to help, so Sam chooses Get involved. The page says that, because volunteers work with youth, every volunteer completes a screening and reference check before starting. Two cards sit under that text. One invites people to give monthly. The other, have a question first, leads to the contact page.

Below the cards is a volunteer sign up form. It has a box for the screening and reference check, which must be ticked before the form is sent.

[pause 2s]

## Donating and asking a question

Sam follows the donate button. The page is called, back a young leader. Sam enters a name and email, and chooses a monthly amount. A note under the amounts says that payments are processed by an external provider, and that Waypoint never sees or stores card details. There is no place on this page to type a card number.

If Sam has a question first, the contact page shows the program's email address, and a form to send a message. Every page keeps the same top menu, so Sam can move between them freely. The people who already have an account use the sign in button there, which leads to the separate sign in page.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

The public site is open to everyone, with no sign in. It shows the program's tracks, modules, badges, cohorts, and impact numbers, and never any young person's details. Volunteers acknowledge the screening check. Donations are finished with an external secure provider. Questions go through the contact page.
