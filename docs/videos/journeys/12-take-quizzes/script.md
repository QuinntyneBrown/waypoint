# 12 · Take quizzes

## Welcome

A quiz is how a young person shows what they have learned in a module. This video follows Amara Bell through one quiz, from the moment it opens to the approved result, and shows why her answers can be trusted.

[pause 2s]

## Before she starts

Amara finished the lessons in Foundations, so the module quiz is open. She opens it, and the page tells her everything she needs to know. There are two questions, it takes about four minutes, the pass mark is seventy percent, and she has two attempts. The page says, attempt one of two.

It also names the badge she can earn by passing, and nothing starts until she presses, start attempt.

[pause 2s]

## One question at a time

Waypoint shows one question at a time. Amara reads, what does Jesus say is the greatest commandment, and picks an answer. A small note says, answer saved. Every answer is saved the moment she gives it, so nothing is lost.

[pause 2s]

## Picking up where she left off

Suppose she is interrupted and reloads the page. She comes back to question two of two, with her earlier choice still selected. On the last question, the button changes to, submit quiz.

[pause 2s]

## Submitting

When she presses submit, Waypoint warns her that the answers cannot be changed for this attempt. Once she confirms, the attempt is scored on the server, and her answers are locked.

For this quiz, the result page says, waiting for mentor approval. Her answers are submitted and locked, and her score will appear after review. She never sees a score that has not been approved.

[pause 2s]

## The result

After Grace, her mentor, approves the attempt, the page shows the approved score, one hundred percent, and the word passed, next to the pass mark. The badge is shown as earned.

Further down, a question review marks each answer as correct or not. Because Amara has an attempt left, a retake quiz button is available, and a retake starts a new attempt.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

The first screen states the questions, time, pass mark, attempts and badge. Answers save as the youth goes, and the quiz can be resumed. Submitting locks the attempt, and the server does the scoring. Where a mentor must approve, the youth sees only the approved score.
