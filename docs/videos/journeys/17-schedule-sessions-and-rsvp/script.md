# 17 · Schedule sessions and RSVP

## Welcome

A session starts as an entry on the calendar, and ends as a record of who came. This video covers the first half. It follows Pat Morgan, a program administrator, as she adds a weekly session, and Amara Bell, a youth, as she tells the program whether she will come.

[pause 2s]

## The new session form

Pat opens the program calendar. Beside the calendar is a form headed new session. It asks for a title, a cohort, a mentor, a date and a time. It also asks how many minutes the session lasts, and where it takes place.

Title, cohort and mentor are required. Pat chooses the cohort and the mentor from lists, so a session always belongs to a real cohort and a real mentor. She writes weekly circle, picks the cohort and Grace Adeyemi, sets the day two days from now, five thirty in the evening, and the program studio as the place.

[pause 2s]

## A weekly session

This session should happen every week, so Pat turns on the repeat weekly option. Waypoint will create a session for every week, starting on that date and ending on the cohort's last day of term. The last day is included, and no session is created after it.

She presses Schedule session. The page confirms that weekly sessions were scheduled through the cohort term. Pat filters the calendar to that cohort, and the agenda lists the new sessions, each with its time, place, mentor and cohort. Each one has a Take attendance button, so the mentor can take attendance on the day without searching for anything.

[pause 2s]

## What Amara sees

Now Amara opens her schedule page. It lists the upcoming sessions for her cohort. Each shows its title, the date, the time, how long it lasts, and where it takes place. The weekly circle is there, with the place Pat chose.

Beside each session is a small reply form, called your RSVP. Amara can answer in one of three ways. Going, maybe, or can't make it.

[pause 2s]

## Replying

Amara thinks she may be late, so she chooses maybe. She writes a note to her mentor, I may arrive a few minutes late. Then she ticks, remind me before this session, and presses Save RSVP.

The page says RSVP saved. It shows her current response, maybe, and the time her reminder is set for. The answer is stored, so when she comes back, the form still shows it. Her mentor can see it too. If plans change, Amara can come back and choose can't make it, which turns the reminder off.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

A session needs a title, a cohort and a mentor, plus a date, a time and a place. Repeat weekly creates one session for each week through the cohort's last day of term. New sessions show on the calendar straight away, with a link to attendance. A youth answers going, maybe, or can't make it, adds a note if she likes, and can ask for a reminder.
