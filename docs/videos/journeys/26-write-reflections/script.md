# 26 · Write reflections

## Welcome

After a lesson, Waypoint invites a young person to stop and think. This video follows Amara Bell from finishing a lesson to a submitted reflection, and shows how she decides who reads what she writes.

[pause 2s]

## The reflection page

Amara marks her lesson complete, and Waypoint opens a page headed, Reflect on Living the Word. Under the title it says, take your time. You can save this as a private draft.

A box called, think about it, repeats a question from the lesson. Where can you practise neighbour love this week? Below it, she is asked, how are you feeling? She can pick hopeful, thoughtful, unsure, or challenged. Then there is a large box for her reflection, and a smaller one called, one action I will take, which is optional.

[pause 2s]

## What is required

Suppose Amara presses submit with nothing written. The page tells her what is missing. It says, reflection text is required, and it asks her to choose how she is feeling.

A note by the text box explains the rule. Required when you submit. Drafts can be unfinished. So she can save a half written thought, but she cannot submit one.

[pause 2s]

## Who can read it

Near the bottom is a box called, share with my mentor. It is off by default. The note says, when it is off, this reflection is private to you. Turn it on only if you want your assigned mentor to read it and respond during check ins.

The choice is hers, one reflection at a time. Next to the submit button there is also a button to save a draft.

[pause 2s]

## Leaving and coming back

Amara writes a few lines, chooses thoughtful, and then decides to leave. Waypoint warns her that she has unsaved content, and lets her save it as a draft before she goes.

When she comes back, a message says, draft restored. Her words and her feeling are where she left them.

[pause 2s]

## Submitting

When she is ready, she turns on share with my mentor, because she would like Grace to see this one, and presses submit reflection. The page says, reflection submitted.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

A reflection needs a feeling and some text to submit, and the action is optional. Sharing with a mentor is off unless the youth turns it on. Leaving with unsaved words brings a warning, and a saved draft is restored on return.
