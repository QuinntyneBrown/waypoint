# 14 · View journey and progress

## Welcome

A young person should always know where they are, what they have finished, and what comes next. Waypoint answers those questions on three pages. This video follows Amara Bell as she checks her journey, her badges, and her progress.

[pause 2s]

## My Journey

Amara chooses My Journey from the menu. The page says, see what you have completed and what unlocks next.

At the top, a banner tells her where she is. It says, you are in Foundations now, and a bar shows her progress through the module. Right now it reads thirty three percent.

[pause 2s]

## The roadmap

Under that is her roadmap. Each module in her track is a card, in order. Foundations is marked in progress, with a line that says, one of three required items complete. A button, continue module, takes her back to her work.

The next card, Deeper Practice, is marked locked, and it says why. Complete Foundations. A module opens only when the one before it is finished, and the server decides this, not the screen. So Amara can see what is ahead, but she cannot skip the steps in between.

[pause 2s]

## The badge collection

Further down is a badge collection. Each badge has a simple count. First Steps says earned. Code Starter says, zero of two, because she has not yet finished the self checks it needs.

A button at the bottom, view badge details, opens the full badges page.

[pause 2s]

## The badges page

The badges page is called Badges, and says that every badge marks progress she has made. Earned badges show what they are for and the date she earned them. Her First Steps badge was for completing her first lesson.

Below it, badges still to unlock say what is needed. A timeline of milestones points to the one she is working toward. The counts on this page come from the same source as the journey page, so they always agree.

[pause 2s]

## My progress

For the numbers, Amara opens My progress. Its overview shows four figures. Overall progress is fifty percent, her quiz average is eighty two percent, she has done one of two lessons, and her attendance is fifty percent.

Below them, leadership competencies show a level and a percentage. Serving others is proficient, at seventy five percent. These levels come from her mentor's assessments, so they are the mentor's judgement and not something she scores herself. Tabs for quizzes and attendance hold the detail.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

My Journey shows modules as done, in progress, or locked, with a count of required items. Locked modules explain what must be finished first. Badges show what they are for, or what is still needed. My progress gathers progress, quiz average, lessons and attendance in one place, and competency levels come from the mentor.
