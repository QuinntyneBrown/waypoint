# 10 · Submit assignments

## Welcome

An assignment is how a young person shows what they have learned by doing something. This video follows Amara Bell from reading the brief, to sending her work, to a mentor's request for changes, and finally to approval.

[pause 2s]

## Reading the brief

Amara opens an assignment called Neighbour Love Project. The page shows the task. Describe one practical act of neighbour love, and include evidence if you can. It shows the due date and time, and a list called what you can submit. A written response, a photo in J P G or P N G format, an evidence link, and a file in P D F format.

Under that is a rubric preview. It lists what the mentor will look for, with the points. Amara sees one criterion, connects scripture to a practical action, worth ten points. So she knows how her work will be judged before she starts.

[pause 2s]

## Checks before sending

Further down is the form. Waypoint checks files as she chooses them. If Amara picks a text file as her photo, the page says, choose a J P G or P N G image, and nothing is uploaded.

Photos and P D F files can be up to ten megabytes. And a box at the end says, this is my own work and the evidence is truthful. She cannot submit without ticking it.

[pause 2s]

## Sending it

Amara writes a short response about helping a neighbour carry groceries, and ticks the box. She presses submit assignment. Waypoint warns her that the submission will be read only, and asks her to confirm.

[pause 2s]

## Waiting for review

She lands on the submission status page. It shows the title, the label submitted, a copy of what she sent, and a timeline that leads to under review. Her copy can no longer be edited, so what Grace reads is exactly what Amara sent.

[pause 2s]

## When changes are needed

Grace reviews the work and asks for a revision. Amara's label now says, needs revision. A box called mentor feedback shows Grace's note. Add the scripture that guided your action. Below it is a button, revise and resubmit.

Amara presses it. Her earlier answer is already filled in, so she does not start from nothing. She adds Luke ten, verse twenty seven, and resubmits.

[pause 2s]

## Approved

The label goes back to under review, and then Grace approves. The timeline keeps the whole story, from submitted to approved, with nothing hidden. Amara sees Grace's comment, a rubric result of nine out of ten points, and the badge her approval unlocks. Here, it is the Practical Love badge.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

The brief shows the task, the due date, the allowed types and the rubric. Files are checked as they are chosen, and the own work box is required. A submission becomes a read only copy, and every step stays in the timeline. A request for changes comes with a note and a way to resubmit. Approval shows the points, the comment, and any badge unlocked.
