# 08 · Apply or refer

## Welcome

Every young person's journey with Waypoint starts with an application, and anyone can start it. A young person can apply for themselves. A parent or guardian can apply on their behalf. And someone else who knows a young person can refer them. This video follows Ruth Clarke as she applies for her daughter Imani, who is fifteen.

[pause 2s]

## Who is filling this out

Ruth opens the apply page on the public site. She does not need an account. The heading says, join a cohort, and a line underneath says the program is open to young people aged twelve to eighteen.

The first question is, who is filling this out. There are three answers. I am applying for myself. I am a parent or guardian. Or I am referring someone. Ruth picks parent or guardian. Next she names the cohort she is interested in. This one is required. Then she enters Imani's first name, last name, age, and neighbourhood.

[pause 2s]

## Age and guardian details

The form checks the age as soon as it is typed. When Ruth first slips and types twenty, a message appears right under the field. It says applicants must be aged twelve to eighteen inclusive. She corrects it to fifteen.

Because Imani is under eighteen, a section called parent or guardian appears. It says it is required for applicants under eighteen. Ruth gives her own name, her email, and her phone number. Ruth can also pick a track, or leave it on no preference yet, and she explains in her own words why Imani is interested.

[pause 2s]

## Consent and privacy

At the bottom are two boxes. One confirms that a parent or guardian consents to participation. The other says the person has read the privacy notice. Both are required.

If Ruth presses submit application before ticking them, nothing is sent. A short message appears under each box, asking her to acknowledge participation consent, and to acknowledge the privacy notice. She ticks both and submits again.

[pause 2s]

## Application received

The page changes to a confirmation. It says application received. Waypoint will review the application and reach out with next steps. And it makes one thing clear. Imani does not have an account yet. An account is set up only once she is accepted into a cohort.

The same form serves a referrer. Someone who picks, I am referring someone, fills in the same details about the young person, and the same consent and privacy boxes apply.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

The form is public, and nobody needs an account to use it. It asks who is filling it out, and the answer can be the young person, a parent or guardian, or a referrer. Ages must be twelve to eighteen. Anyone under eighteen needs guardian details. Both the consent box and the privacy box must be ticked. And no account exists until the application is accepted.
