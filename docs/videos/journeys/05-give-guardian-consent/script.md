# 05 · Give guardian consent

## Welcome

A young person under the age of majority cannot start lessons until a parent or guardian gives permission. This video follows one family through that whole journey. The young person is stopped, the guardian signs, and later the guardian can look back at what was signed, or take the permission back.

[pause 2s]

## Imani is stopped

Meet Imani Clarke. She has just joined the Fall cohort, and her mother, Ruth, has not signed the consent yet. Imani signs in. Instead of her home page, she sees a screen headed, a guardian needs to sign off.

It explains that before she can start lessons, a parent or guardian needs to give participation consent, and that it only takes a minute. There are three buttons. Notify my guardian. My guardian can sign now. And sign out.

[pause 2s]

## The secure link

Imani presses Notify my guardian. A green message says that a secure consent link was sent by email. The address is partly hidden, so the screen never gives away the full email.

Ruth receives an email with a one-time link. If she were sitting next to Imani, Imani could choose, my guardian can sign now, instead. That signs Imani out and takes Ruth to the guardian sign in page.

[pause 2s]

## Ruth signs

Ruth opens the link. She does not need an account for this form. The page names the young person, Imani Clarke, and shows when the link expires.

Ruth types her full name and her relationship, Mother. She ticks the box that gives permission for Imani to take part. That is the one required choice.

Below it are optional media choices. Ruth allows Waypoint to use photos or video, and she picks group photos only. Each choice is recorded on its own. Then she types her full name again as her signature, and presses sign and submit consent.

A card says, consent recorded, and gives a reference number that starts with G C. A copy is sent to her email, and Imani's access updates automatically. She does not need anyone to unlock her by hand.

[pause 2s]

## Looking back, or withdrawing

Later, Ruth signs in to the guardian portal and opens the Consent page. The same record is there, marked Active. It shows who signed, and how they are related to Imani. It shows the date, the method, which is a typed e-signature, how long the consent is valid, and the reference number. She can email herself a copy.

If Ruth ever changes her mind, she presses Withdraw consent and confirms. The badge turns to Withdrawn, and the Withdraw button disappears. The record stays on the page, so the history is clear.

From then on, a protected action by Imani, such as finishing a lesson, sends her back to the consent needed screen. If Ruth signs again, the same session is unblocked.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

A young person without participation consent is held on one screen and cannot reach any learning. The guardian signs through a secure link, with no account needed. Media choices are optional and recorded one by one. The consent record stays visible in the guardian portal, and withdrawing it takes effect from then on.
