# 078 · Capturing and managing guardian consent

## Welcome

Waypoint records guardian consent in two parts. Participation consent is required. Media consent, for photos and video, is optional. This video shows how a guardian signs, what Waypoint stores, and what happens when consent is withdrawn.

[pause 2s]

## Signing the form

Ruth Clarke has received a secure link for her daughter, Imani Clarke. She opens it, and sees a form that names Imani and shows when the link expires.

Three things are required. Ruth types her full name. She types her relationship to Imani, which is Mother. And she ticks the box that gives permission for Imani to take part. Then she types her full name once more as her signature.

Media consent is optional. Ruth ticks the box that allows Waypoint to use photos or video. Then she chooses what that covers. All photos and video, group photos only, or first-name captions. Ruth picks group photos only. Each of these choices is recorded on its own.

She presses the button to sign and submit.

[pause 2s]

## What Waypoint stores

A card says, consent recorded, with a reference number that starts with G C. Waypoint stores the name of the signer, the method, which is a typed e-signature, the date and time, a unique reference number, and how long the consent is valid. A copy of the record is emailed to Ruth.

Later, Ruth signs in to the guardian portal. On the Consent page she finds the same record, marked Active, and her media choices ticked exactly as she set them.

[pause 2s]

## Changing her mind

If Ruth withdraws her consent, she confirms, and the badge turns to Withdrawn. The withdrawal is recorded. Anything that depended on that consent, such as using Imani's photos, stops from that moment onward. It does not reach back and undo the past.

Some organizations ask for fresh consent each term. When the policy says so, consent from an earlier term does not cover a new term, and the guardian is asked to sign again.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Participation consent needs a name, a relationship, a ticked permission, and a typed signature. Media consent is optional, and each choice is recorded independently. Waypoint stores who signed, how, when, a reference number, and the validity, and emails a copy. Withdrawal is recorded and applies from then on.
