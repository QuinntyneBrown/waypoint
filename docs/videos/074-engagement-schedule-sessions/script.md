# 074 · Scheduling sessions

## Welcome

Before anyone can take attendance, a session has to exist. This video shows how staff add sessions to the Waypoint calendar, including a weekly session that repeats through the whole term.

[pause 2s]

## The new session form

Meet Pat Morgan, a program administrator. Pat opens the program calendar. Beside the calendar is a form, headed new session. It asks for a title, a cohort, a mentor, a date and a time. It also asks for how many minutes the session lasts, and where it takes place.

Title, cohort and mentor are required. Staff choose the cohort and the mentor from lists, so a session always belongs to a real cohort and a real mentor.

[pause 2s]

## A weekly session

Pat fills in the form. The title is weekly circle. The cohort is the one Pat picked from the list, the mentor is Grace Adeyemi, the day is two days from now, and the time is five thirty in the evening. The place is the program studio.

This session should happen every week, so Pat turns on the repeat weekly option. Waypoint will create a session for every week, starting on that date and ending on the cohort's last day of term. The last day is included, and no session is created after it.

[pause 2s]

## Scheduled

Pat presses schedule. The page confirms with a message that weekly sessions were scheduled through the cohort term. Pat filters the calendar to that cohort, and the agenda now lists the new sessions, each with its time, its place, its mentor and its cohort.

Each one links to attendance, so the mentor can take attendance on the day without searching for anything.

If a session is not weekly, Pat simply leaves the option off, and one session is created.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

A session needs a title, a cohort and a mentor, plus a date, time and place. Repeat weekly creates one session for each week through the cohort's last day of term, and none after it. New sessions show up on the calendar straight away, with a link to attendance.
