# 002 · Accepting an invitation

## Welcome

In Waypoint, nobody signs themselves up. People join because someone invited them. This video follows one invitation from the email to the first screen inside Waypoint, and shows what happens when a link can no longer be used.

[pause 2s]

## Opening the link

Pat Morgan, a program administrator, has invited Amara Bell to join as a youth. Amara receives an email with a personal link. She opens it and sees a page that says, join Waypoint as youth. Under the heading it shows the invitation is for her address, but with part of it hidden, so a stranger who finds the link learns very little.

The link contains a long random secret. Waypoint keeps only a scrambled form of that secret, not the secret itself.

[pause 2s]

## Setting up the account

Amara fills in three things. First, the display name she wants people to see. Second, a password, which she types twice. The page asks for at least eight characters, with a letter and a number. There is an eye button if she wants to check what she typed. Third, she ticks a box to say she agrees to follow the community guidelines. Without that tick, she cannot join.

When she presses join Waypoint, her account is created with the role from the invitation. If Pat also picked a cohort and a mentor, those come with it. Then Amara goes straight to the first-time setup, where she can add her details or skip for now.

[pause 2s]

## Links that no longer work

An invitation link works once. If Amara opens the same link again, the page says, this invite has already been used, and points her to sign in with the password she created.

A link also stops working if it expires, or if staff revoke it. The page says so plainly, and for a link that is no longer active it offers a button, request a new invite. The page tells the visitor that invite links are single use and expire after fourteen days.

Staff are not stuck either. They can resend an invitation, which gives a fresh link, or revoke it, which stops the old link straight away.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

An invitation is a personal link. To accept it, you choose a display name, a password, and agree to the community guidelines. The link works once. Expired or revoked links do not work, and the page offers a way to ask for a new invite.
