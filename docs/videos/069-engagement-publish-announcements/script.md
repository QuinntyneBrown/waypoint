# 069 · Publishing announcements

## Welcome

Some news is for everyone, or for one group. This video shows how staff publish an announcement in Waypoint, who receives it, and how staff can tell whether it was read.

[pause 2s]

## Composing an announcement

Meet Pat Morgan, a program administrator. Pat opens the announcement administration page. It says, send program or cohort updates and follow recipient read-through.

On the left is the compose form. Pat writes a title and a message. Then Pat chooses who should get it. The audience can be the whole organization, or a single cohort. The recipients can be youth, youth and guardians together, guardians, mentors, or everyone.

[pause 2s]

## Send now or schedule

At the bottom is the delivery choice. Send now delivers the announcement straight away. Schedule for later asks for a date and time, and Waypoint sends it then. Pat schedules a note for guardians about trip details, to go out tomorrow. The page confirms it, and the announcement shows in the list with a scheduled label.

Then Pat sends a second announcement, for one cohort and for youth only, right now. It appears in the list with a sent label, and the cohort and audience beside it.

[pause 2s]

## What recipients see

Now Amara Bell, a youth in that cohort, opens her announcements page. She sees the new one, marked new. Only announcements meant for her audience and role appear here, so the guardian note never reaches her.

Amara presses mark all read, and the new label goes away.

[pause 2s]

## Tracking reads

Back on Pat's page, the sent announcement started with zero of one read. Now it says one of one read, and one hundred percent read. Every time a recipient opens an announcement, Waypoint records it, and shows staff the read count and the overall read rate. That tells Pat whether the message actually reached people.

Announcement text is also checked and encoded before it is shown to anyone.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Pick an audience and a role, then send now or schedule. Each person sees only the announcements meant for them. Unread items are marked new, and mark all read clears them. Staff can follow how many recipients have read each announcement.
