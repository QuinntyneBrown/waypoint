# 064 · Recording session attendance

## Welcome

Volunteers help run sessions, and one of their jobs is to note who came. This video follows Sam Whitfield, a volunteer, as Sam takes attendance for today's session. The attendance page is built to be quick, and it shows a volunteer only what the job needs.

[pause 2s]

## The roster

Sam opens today's session and presses take attendance. The page is headed take attendance, and a line underneath says only names and present or absent attendance are shown. There are three young people on the roster, Amara, David, and Imani. Beside each name are two buttons, present and absent.

Notice what is missing. There is no progress, no notes, and no flag about anyone's wellbeing. A volunteer sees a name and nothing more. That is deliberate. Volunteers are trusted to help with a session, but the rest of a young person's record is not theirs to see.

[pause 2s]

## Marking everyone

At the top, three small tiles keep count. They show how many are marked, how many are present, and how many are absent. Right now it says zero of three marked.

Most sessions have mostly present youth, so Sam presses all present. Every name is marked at once. Then Sam presses absent beside David, who is away today. The tiles now read three of three marked, two present, and one absent. If Sam makes a mistake, the clear button starts again.

At the bottom, a line says unsaved changes. The save button stays switched off until every young person has been marked, so a half-finished list cannot be saved by accident.

[pause 2s]

## Saving

Sam presses save attendance. A message says attendance saved, and the line at the bottom changes to changes saved. The record is now stored, and still only names and marks are involved.

[pause 2s]

## When the connection drops

Sessions do not always have a good signal. Suppose Sam changes a mark while offline. A bar across the top says, you are offline, changes will save when you reconnect. Sam presses save anyway, and Waypoint says saved offline, one attendance update waiting to sync. A small badge also shows one pending sync.

When the connection returns, nothing more is needed. The message changes to all attendance changes synced. The update is sent once, not twice, so no marks are lost or doubled. If someone else saved the same session while Sam was offline, Waypoint does not guess. It asks Sam to choose which attendance to keep.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

A volunteer sees names only, with a present button and an absent button for each. The all present button speeds things up, and the tiles show the running count. Save is available once everyone is marked. Offline changes are queued and synced once the connection is back, and a clash with someone else's save is shown clearly for Sam to resolve. Anything outside a volunteer's own session is refused.
