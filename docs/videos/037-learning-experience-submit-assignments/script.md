# 037 · Submitting an assignment

## Welcome

An assignment is how a young person shows what they have learned by doing something. This video follows a youth from reading the brief to a submission that is safely with the mentor.

[pause 2s]

## The brief

Amara opens an assignment called Neighbour Love Project. The page shows the task. Describe one practical act of neighbour love, and include evidence if you can. It shows the due date and time, and a list called what you can submit. A written response, a photo in J P G or P N G format, an evidence link, and a file in P D F format.

Under that is a rubric preview. It lists what the mentor will look for, with the points. Amara sees one criterion, connects scripture to a practical action, worth ten points. So she knows how the work will be judged before she starts.

[pause 2s]

## Checks before sending

Further down is the form. If Amara presses submit before ticking the box that says, this is my own work and the evidence is truthful, the page asks her to confirm it.

Waypoint also checks files as she chooses them. When she picks a text file as her photo, the page says, choose a J P G or P N G image, and nothing is uploaded. Photos and P D F files can be up to ten megabytes.

[pause 2s]

## Ready to send

Now Amara picks a real photo. A message says, photo ready, with the file name. She writes a short response about helping a neighbour carry groceries, and ticks the box that says it is her own work.

She presses submit assignment. Waypoint warns her that the submission will be read only, and asks her to confirm.

[pause 2s]

## Submitted

She lands on the submission status page. It shows the title, the status submitted, her submitted copy, and a timeline that leads to under review. From this moment she cannot edit the submission. If the mentor asks for changes, there is a separate way to revise it, and that is covered in the next videos.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

The brief shows the task, the due date, the allowed types and the rubric. The form enforces required fields and the own work box. Photos must be J P G or P N G, and files are checked as they are chosen. After submitting, the work is read only and goes to the mentor.
