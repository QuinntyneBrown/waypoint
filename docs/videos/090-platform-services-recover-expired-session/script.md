# 090 · Coming back after a session expires

## Welcome

Sooner or later, everyone walks away from a screen. This video shows what Waypoint does when a person has been away so long that their session has ended, and how it protects the work they were in the middle of.

[pause 2s]

## Work in progress

Amara Bell is writing a reflection on her lesson. She has chosen how she is feeling, thoughtful, and she has typed one sentence about what she learned. Then she gets called away, and the computer sits untouched.

Waypoint does not leave a session open forever. When Amara has been inactive for too long, Waypoint ends her session on its own.

[pause 2s]

## You have been signed out

When Amara comes back and acts, she sees a page headed, you have been signed out. A small label above it says, session expired. The page explains that for her security, Waypoint signed her out after a period of inactivity. It also says something reassuring. Any work in progress has been saved.

There is one button, sign in again. She presses it and signs in with her email and password, the same way as always.

[pause 2s]

## Back where she left off

After she signs in, Amara does not land on a general home page. Waypoint takes her straight back to the reflection she was writing. A green message at the top says, draft restored after session expiry. Her mood is still chosen, and her sentence is still in the box.

The saved draft belongs to the person who wrote it. If someone else signed in on that computer, they would not receive Amara's words.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

An expired session ends with a calm page, not an error. The page says what happened, why it happened, and that the work was saved. One button leads back to sign in, and after signing in the draft returns to the page where it was written.
