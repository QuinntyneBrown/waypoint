# 081 · Waiting to be placed in a cohort

## Welcome

Some young people sign in before their mentor has placed them in a cohort. This short video shows what Waypoint tells them while they wait, and what they can do about it.

[pause 2s]

## The waiting state

Meet Amara. She has just signed in for the first time. Her account is active, but she has not been placed in a cohort yet. Waypoint does not leave her guessing, and it does not show her a blank page.

Her home page opens with a friendly welcome that uses her first name. Below it is a clear message. It says, you are not in a cohort yet. Then it explains. Your mentor will place you in a cohort soon. Once you are placed, your lessons and journey unlock here.

So Amara learns three things at a glance. She is not in trouble. Nothing is broken. And her lessons will appear on this same page once the placement happens.

The program rule behind this is simple. Until a youth is placed, program content stays locked. The home page is where Waypoint explains why.

[pause 2s]

## Reaching out

Under the message there is one button. It says, message your mentor. Amara presses it, and Waypoint takes her to her messages.

Here the screen is honest about where things stand. It says, your mentor has not been assigned yet. This conversation will open when your placement is ready. In other words, she cannot send a message into the void. When a mentor is assigned, the conversation opens.

That is a good thing for program staff to know. If a young person says they are stuck, you can check two things. Has the youth been placed in a cohort, and has a mentor been assigned? Placement is done by the program, not by the youth, and the youth sees the page update after it happens.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

A youth who has not been placed sees a plain explanation on the home page, with the words, you are not in a cohort yet. Lessons and journey stay locked until placement. The message your mentor button leads to messages, which open once a mentor is assigned.
