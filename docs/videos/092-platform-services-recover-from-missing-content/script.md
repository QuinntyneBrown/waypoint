# 092 · When a page cannot be found

## Welcome

Links get old. Pages move, bookmarks go stale, and people mistype addresses. This video shows what Waypoint does when someone asks for something that is not there, and how it gets them back on track.

[pause 2s]

## A link that leads nowhere

Pat Morgan, a program administrator, has an old bookmark to a cohort page. She clicks it, and the page no longer exists.

Waypoint shows a page headed, page not found. A small label above it says, error four oh four. The text is friendly and short. The page you are looking for moved or never existed, and Waypoint will get you back on track.

There is no stack of technical words and no blame. It simply says the page is not there. Pat is still signed in, and nothing she did was wrong. A missing page does not sign anyone out.

[pause 2s]

## Back to safety

The page has one button, back to safety. Pat presses it, and Waypoint takes her to the home made for her role, the director dashboard. From there she can find the cohort the normal way, through the menu on the left.

The destination depends on who is asking. A signed-in person goes to the home for their role. Someone who is not signed in goes to the sign-in page instead.

[pause 2s]

## When the data is missing

The same page also appears when a page exists but the thing it asks for does not. For example, a screen asks the server for a record, and the server answers that it cannot find it. Waypoint shows the same page not found, so the person always sees one familiar, calm explanation.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

A missing page ends at a plain page not found. The page offers one clear way back, to the home for the person's role, or to sign in. A missing record is treated the same way as a missing page.
