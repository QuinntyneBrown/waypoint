# 071 · Recording attendance

## Welcome

Attendance is one of the most important records a youth program keeps. This video shows how a mentor records who came to a session in Waypoint, and what happens to that record afterwards.

[pause 2s]

## The roster

Meet Grace Adeyemi, a mentor. From the calendar, she opens a session and presses take attendance. The page shows the session title, the cohort, the day, the time and the place. Below it is the roster, with one row for each youth. In our example there is one youth, Amara Bell.

Across the top is a running tally. It says how many youth are marked out of how many, and then counts present, late, excused and absent. Right now, nobody is marked.

[pause 2s]

## Marking each youth

Each row has four buttons: present, late, excused and absent. Amara arrived a little behind the others, so Grace presses late. The tally updates straight away, and now says, one of one marked, and one late.

Next to the buttons is a staff note. Grace types a short reason, transit delay, and Amara joined ready to take part. The note is stored with the session, and is visible to the mentor and the director.

For a big group, there is a faster way. Press all present, and everyone is marked present. Grace can then change any single row. Clear puts everything back to unmarked.

[pause 2s]

## Saving

When the roster looks right, Grace presses save attendance. The page confirms it with the words, attendance saved. From that moment, the session has an attendance record.

That record feeds the attendance rates that staff review later, and the streak rules for badges. If the session is saved a second time, Waypoint checks that nobody else has changed it in the meantime, so one person's edit cannot quietly overwrite another's. A program director can also edit attendance on a mentor's behalf.

Volunteers record attendance too. Their sheet is simpler, with only present and absent.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Each youth is marked present, late, excused or absent. A running tally shows how many are marked. All present and clear speed up a big roster. A note can sit beside each mark. Saving makes the record count toward attendance rates and badge streaks.
