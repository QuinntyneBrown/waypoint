# 083 · Changing your password

## Welcome

Sometimes a person wants a new password, even though nothing has gone wrong. This video shows how Waypoint lets someone change it from inside their own account, and which checks it makes along the way.

[pause 2s]

## Where to find it

Meet David. He is a youth in the Fall cohort, and he wants a stronger password. He opens his profile settings from the side menu. Below his profile details there is a section called, change password.

It has three fields. Current password. New password. And confirm new password. The current password is there for a reason. Waypoint asks for it so that someone who walks up to an unlocked computer cannot change the password without knowing it.

[pause 2s]

## Rules on the screen

David starts typing a new password. It is only five letters long. Under the field, a hint states the rule. Use at least eight characters, including a letter and a number. While the password breaks the rule, the change password button stays switched off. He cannot press it by accident.

He types a longer one, but when he confirms it, he makes a typo. Right away, the page says, passwords do not match, and the button stays off. He fixes the typo. Now the two fields agree, the rule is met, and the button turns on.

There is also a small eye icon in the new password field, so David can look at what he typed before he commits to it.

[pause 2s]

## Saving the change

He presses change password. Waypoint checks his current password. The page shows a short message. Password changed. The form clears, so nothing sensitive is left on screen.

The program requirement says one more thing. When a password changes, the person's other sessions are signed out. So if David had left himself signed in on a shared computer, that session ends.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

The change password form asks for the current password, a new one, and a confirmation. The button stays off until the new password follows the rule and both copies match. And a successful change ends the person's other sessions.
