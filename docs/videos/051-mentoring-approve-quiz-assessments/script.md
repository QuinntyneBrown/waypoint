# 051 · Approving quiz scores

## Welcome

When a young person finishes a quiz in Waypoint, the quiz is scored automatically. But that score does not count yet. A mentor looks at it first. This video shows how a mentor approves a quiz score, or changes it, and why the young person only ever sees the approved result.

[pause 2s]

## The review queue

Grace is a mentor. She opens Reviews, and sees everything waiting for her. Each line names the youth, the kind of work, and whether it is overdue. Amara has just finished a quiz called Living the Word, and it sits in the list with a Review button.

Grace presses Review. She sees the question, the answer Amara picked, and which answer was correct. On the right, the screen shows the auto score, forty percent, and the pass mark, seventy percent. A note says it is below the pass mark.

[pause 2s]

## Approve or override

Grace has two choices. If she leaves the override box empty, pressing Approve score accepts the forty percent as it is. Or she can type her own score. Perhaps Amara explained her answer in person, and Grace wants to give her more credit.

The score must be a whole number from zero to one hundred. If Grace types one hundred and one, Waypoint stops her and says so, and nothing is saved.

So she types seventy five, and presses Approve score. Waypoint asks her to confirm, and then it saves.

Now the page shows an approval record. It lists the auto score, forty percent, and the approved score, seventy five percent, with Grace's name and the time. Both numbers are kept, and the change is written to the audit log, so there is always a trail showing what the machine said and what the mentor decided.

[pause 2s]

## Approve all

On a busy week there may be many quizzes waiting. Back on Reviews, a button says Approve all quiz scores, with a count beside it. Grace presses it and confirms, and a message says how many quiz assessments were approved. Each one is recorded, and the quizzes leave the list.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

A quiz score does not count until a mentor approves it. Only the approved score counts toward progress and averages, and it is the only score the youth sees. A mentor can accept the auto score or enter an override from zero to one hundred. Both numbers are kept for audit. Approve all clears every pending quiz in the mentor's scope in one action.
