# 073 · Replying to a session

## Welcome

Mentors plan sessions around the youth who plan to come. This video shows how a youth tells the program whether they will attend a session, adds a short note for their mentor, and asks for a reminder.

[pause 2s]

## My schedule

Meet Amara Bell, a youth. She opens her schedule page. It says, my schedule, and lists the upcoming sessions for her cohort. Each one shows its title, the day and time, and where it takes place. In our example, the community project workshop is coming up, at the Waypoint community hub.

Beside each session is a small reply form, called your RSVP. Amara can answer in one of three ways: going, maybe, or can't make it.

[pause 2s]

## Answering with a note

Amara thinks she may be late, so she chooses maybe. Below that is an optional box, labelled note to your mentor. She writes, I may arrive a few minutes late. The note is meant for her mentor, so Grace Adeyemi knows what to expect.

Below the note is a checkbox, remind me before this session. Amara ticks it. Then she presses save RSVP.

[pause 2s]

## Saved, and remembered

The page says, RSVP saved. It shows her current answer, maybe, and the time her reminder is set for. Her answer is stored, so if she leaves and comes back, the form still shows maybe, her note, and the ticked reminder. The mentor can see her answer, too.

The reminder is a notification that Waypoint schedules ahead of the session, and it arrives in her notification feed like any other.

[pause 2s]

## Changing her mind

Plans change. Amara goes back and chooses can't make it. The reminder checkbox clears and turns off, because there is no session to be reminded about. She presses save RSVP again, and the page shows her new answer.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

A youth answers going, maybe or can't make it. A note can go to the mentor, and a reminder can be set. The answer is saved, and can be changed later. Choosing can't make it turns the reminder off.
