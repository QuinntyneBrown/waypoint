# 061 · Reading announcements

## Welcome

The program has news for families. Showcase dates, pick-up changes, reminders. This video shows where a guardian reads that news, how Waypoint shows what is new, and what the page looks like when there is nothing yet.

[pause 2s]

## The news page

Pat Morgan, the program administrator, has sent two announcements to the guardians of the Fall cohort. One is called Family showcase this Friday. The other is called Pick-up arrangements for next week. Pat has also sent a third message, meant only for the youth. It tells them to bring their laptops.

Ruth Clarke is a guardian in that cohort. She signs in and opens News from the menu. The page is called News and announcements. She sees the two messages meant for guardians. The youth message is not there. A guardian only sees announcements that were sent to guardians, or to their child's cohort.

[pause 2s]

## Unread and mark all read

Each announcement that Ruth has not opened carries a small green badge that says Unread. Each one shows its date, and its message in full, so she can read it right on the page.

At the top right there is a button, Mark all read. When Ruth presses it, the badges go away. The button goes away too, because there is nothing left to mark. Waypoint remembers this for Ruth only. Other guardians still see their own unread messages.

[pause 2s]

## When there is nothing yet

Not every guardian has news waiting. If no announcement has been sent, the page does not show a blank space. It says, no announcements yet, and adds that program and cohort news will appear here. While the page is loading, it says so as well.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Guardians see only the announcements sent to guardians or to their child's cohort. Unread ones are marked, and Mark all read clears them for that guardian alone. An empty page explains itself.
