# 057 · Recording a check-in

## Welcome

Mentoring happens in conversations. A phone call, a visit, a quick chat after a session. Waypoint gives mentors a place to write these conversations down, and to decide who gets to see each one. This video shows how Grace records a check-in with Amara, and how privacy is handled.

[pause 2s]

## The check-in journal

Grace opens Check-ins. On the left is a form to log a new check-in. On the right is the history for the youth she has chosen. Each entry in the history shows the type, the date, and a label that says whether the note is private or shared. It also shows the follow-up date, if there is one.

The older entry here is a private mentor note. Only Grace, and the staff who are allowed to, can read it.

[pause 2s]

## Writing it down

To log a new check-in, Grace needs three things. The youth, the date, and the type of conversation. Grace picks a call. Then she can add more. How the youth seemed, which Waypoint calls engagement or mood. The goals they discussed. Any prayer requests. Action items. And a date for the next follow-up.

[pause 2s]

## Who can see it

Under the form, there is a switch. Share this check-in with the youth and guardian. It starts off. A check-in is a private mentor note unless Grace chooses otherwise.

Prayer requests get extra care. They stay with the mentor, even when other parts of the check-in are shared. The only way a prayer request is shared is when Grace switches on sharing herself. And when she does, Waypoint says so in plain words. All entered fields, including prayer requests, will be visible to the youth and their linked guardian.

There is one more box. Guardian was informed. If Grace has already spoken with Amara's guardian, she ticks it, and that is recorded on the check-in.

[pause 2s]

## Saving and finding it again

Grace presses Save check-in, and a message confirms it. The new entry appears at the top of the history. It carries the label Shared, and the note that the guardian was informed.

Later, Grace can narrow the history. She can filter by youth, and by the type of conversation, so a call is easy to find among the visits.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Every check-in needs a youth, a date, and a type. Sharing is off by default, so a check-in is private unless the mentor decides to share it. Prayer requests are mentor-only unless sharing is switched on. A shared check-in shows on the youth's progress page, and to the guardian where it applies. The history can be filtered by youth and by type.
