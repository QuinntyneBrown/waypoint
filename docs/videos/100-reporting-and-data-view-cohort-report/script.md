# 100 · The cohort report

## Welcome

A cohort is a group of young people who learn together. When a funder or a board asks how a cohort is doing, the answer is the cohort report. This video shows what it contains and how to read it.

[pause 2s]

## Generating the report

Pat Morgan, a program administrator, opens the reports hub and chooses the cohort report tab. There are two menus. Cohort report selection is the cohort Pat wants to read. Compare with is what to measure it against. If Pat leaves it alone, Waypoint compares with the program average and the previous term. Pat can also pick another cohort instead.

Pat chooses a cohort and presses generate cohort report.

[pause 2s]

## Reading the report

The report opens with the cohort name and a headline line. How many are enrolled, how many are active, and how many are at risk. Under it are three figures. Average completion, attendance, and retention.

Below those, the report is split into three cards. Module completion shows how far the cohort has got in each module. Youth summary lists every young person with a status and their own completion, so David Osei, who is falling behind, shows up as at risk. Pat can see at a glance who needs a mentor's attention.

The third card is comparisons. Each comparison shows completion, attendance, and retention, so Pat can see whether the cohort is ahead of the program average, or behind it.

[pause 2s]

## Who can see it

Like every report, the cohort report is scoped to the person asking. A mentor sees only their own youth. And the export buttons at the top of the hub export this report, exactly as it appears.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Pick a cohort, choose what to compare it with, and generate. The report shows enrolment, completion, attendance, retention, module progress, and a youth summary with status. Comparisons show how the cohort stands against the program average, another cohort, or the previous term.
