# 014 · Contacting the program

## Welcome

Not everyone is ready to apply. Some people just have a question. A guardian wants to know how the program works. A church wants to talk about a partnership. This short video shows the public contact form, and how Waypoint makes sure every message is received.

[pause 2s]

## The contact page

Meet Sam Whitfield. Sam helps at a local church, and wants to ask the program about hosting an evening with a cohort. Sam opens the public site and chooses Contact in the top menu. The page is titled, get in touch. It shows the program's email address and says the program is in Toronto, Ontario, in case Sam would rather write directly.

Below that is the form. There are four fields, and all four are required. Your name. Email. Topic. And message. The topic is chosen from a short list. The page does not ask for an account, and Sam does not need to sign in.

[pause 2s]

## When something is missing

Suppose Sam presses send message too early, with a mistyped email. Waypoint does not send anything. Instead, each field that needs attention gets its own plain message, right beneath it. Enter your name. Enter a valid email address. Enter a message.

Nothing is lost, and nothing is sent half finished. Sam fixes the fields, one at a time, and the messages clear.

[pause 2s]

## Message sent

Sam fills in the name, the email, and the topic, partnership, and writes a short note about the church evening. Then Sam presses send message.

The page now says, message sent. It thanks Sam, and says someone from the team will get back soon. A button leads back to the home page.

Behind the scenes, Waypoint checks the text again on its own side before it records the message, so a form that has been tampered with cannot slip anything unsafe through. What the page shows Sam is only what is true. The message was received.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

The contact form is open to everyone, with no sign in. Name, email, topic, and message are all required. A missing or malformed entry is explained right beside the field. A clear message sent page confirms that the team received it.
