# 042 · The learning journey

## Welcome

A young person should always know where they are, what they have finished, and what comes next. The journey page in Waypoint answers those three questions. This short video walks through it.

[pause 2s]

## My Journey

Meet Amara. She chooses, My Journey, from the menu. The page is called, My Journey, and says, see what you have completed and what unlocks next.

At the top, a banner tells her where she is. It says, you are in Foundations, now, and a bar shows her progress through the module. Right now it reads thirty-three percent.

[pause 2s]

## The roadmap

Under that is a section called, your roadmap. Each module in her track is a card, in order. The first card is Foundations. It carries a label that says, in progress, and a line that says, one of three required items complete. Below it is a button, continue module, which takes her back to her work.

[pause 2s]

## Locked modules

The next card is called, Deeper Practice, and its label says, locked. It also says why, complete Foundations. A module opens only when the one before it is finished, and the server decides this, not the screen. So Amara can see what is ahead, but she cannot skip over the steps in between.

[pause 2s]

## The badge collection

Further down is a badge collection. It shows each badge with a simple count. The First Steps badge says, earned. The Code Starter badge says, zero of two. A button at the bottom, view badge details, opens the full badges page.

These counts come from a single source of truth, so the journey page, the badges page, and the home page always agree.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Modules are shown as done, in progress, or locked, with a count of required items. The current module is highlighted, with a button to continue. Locked modules explain what must be finished first. Badge counts match everywhere in Waypoint.
