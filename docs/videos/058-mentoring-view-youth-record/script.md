# 058 · The youth record

## Welcome

Before a mentor talks to a young person, it helps to see the whole picture. Waypoint gives each assigned youth a record, with everything a mentor needs to prepare, and nothing they should not see. This video opens the record of David Osei.

[pause 2s]

## Opening the record

Grace opens My youth and chooses David. His name is at the top, with his cohort. Under it, a note says this is a mentor-only coaching record.

Right below that, there is a red banner. David is at risk. The banner gives the reason, follow up after two missed sessions, and says who flagged him, and when. So before Grace reads anything else, she knows why he needs attention.

[pause 2s]

## Profile and guardian contact

The record is split into tabs. The first is Profile. It shows David's email, his pronouns if he has given them, and his cohort. Beside it is the guardian contact, with a name, an email, a phone number, and an emergency contact.

Guardian contact is sensitive. Only the assigned mentor and program staff can see it. If a mentor tries to open the record of a youth who is not assigned to them, Waypoint refuses.

[pause 2s]

## Progress, attendance, check-ins

The next three tabs show how David is doing. Progress shows how many lessons he has completed, here one of two. Attendance lists his sessions, with Present or Absent for each, and the date. Here he attended a Leadership Lab, and missed a Community Night.

The Check-ins tab lists the conversations Grace has already had with him, with the type, whether each is private, and the date. A button opens the full journal, where she can log a new one.

[pause 2s]

## Submissions and competencies

The Submissions tab lists David's work and its status. His service reflection has been submitted, and is waiting for review. The Competencies tab shows his current levels, and a button opens the tracker, where Grace can record a new assessment.

So a mentor can go from one record to every coaching action, without hunting for the youth in different places.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

The record has tabs for profile, progress, attendance, check-ins, submissions, and competencies. An at-risk banner gives the reason, who flagged, and when. Guardian contact is for the assigned mentor and staff only. A youth who is not assigned to the mentor cannot be opened. Private check-in detail stays out of the record view.

That completes our look at mentoring.
