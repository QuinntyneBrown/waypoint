# 060 · Keeping contact details up to date

## Welcome

When something happens at a session, the program needs to reach the right adult quickly. This video shows how a guardian keeps their own contact details, and their emergency contact, current in Waypoint.

[pause 2s]

## The contact card

Ruth Clarke signs in as a guardian and opens the Consent page. On the left is a card called Contact details. It holds six fields. Her name. Her email. Her relationship to the young person, which here says Parent. Her phone number. The name of an emergency contact. And the phone number for that emergency contact.

Everything on the card is already filled in with what the program has on file, so Ruth only needs to change what is out of date.

[pause 2s]

## Making a change

Ruth has a new phone number, and she wants a different emergency contact. She types the new phone number. She replaces the emergency contact name with Daniel Clarke, and adds Daniel's phone number.

Then she presses Save contact details. A green message appears at the bottom of the page. It says, contact details saved.

[pause 2s]

## Checking that it stuck

To be sure, Ruth reloads the page. The card comes back with the new phone number, Daniel Clarke as the emergency contact, and his number beside it. The change is stored by Waypoint, not just on her screen.

This card is only ever about Ruth. She can change her own details, and nothing else. If someone tried to open another guardian's details, or a young person's record, Waypoint would refuse the request.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

A guardian edits their own name, relationship, email, phone, and emergency contact in one place. Saving shows a confirmation, and the change is still there after a reload. Nobody can change anyone else's details from this page.
