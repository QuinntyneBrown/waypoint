# 067 · Browsing the program calendar

## Welcome

Every session in Waypoint lives on one calendar. This short video shows how a program administrator, a mentor or a volunteer browses it, and how one click takes them from a session to its attendance sheet.

[pause 2s]

## Three ways to look

Meet Pat Morgan, a program administrator. Pat opens the program calendar. The page says, schedule sessions across cohorts and open their attendance rosters.

There are three views. Month shows the whole month at a glance. Week lays out seven days. Agenda is a simple list of what is coming up. Pat can switch between them with one click, and move forward or back with the previous, today and next buttons.

Mentors and volunteers have a calendar of their own too, and each person only sees what their role allows.

[pause 2s]

## One cohort at a time

A program can run several cohorts at once, so the calendar starts with everything. Pat does not need all of it. There is a calendar cohort filter above the sessions. Pat picks one cohort, and every view shows only that cohort's sessions.

In the agenda, each session is a card. It shows the day, the time and the place. Underneath, it names the cohort, the mentor, and how many youth are expected. In our example, Grace Adeyemi is leading a community project workshop, and one youth is on the roster.

[pause 2s]

## From session to attendance

Every card has a button on the right. If nobody has recorded attendance yet, it says, take attendance. If attendance has already been saved, it says, edit attendance. Either way, it opens the roster for that exact session, so there is no hunting for the right class on the right day.

That is the link between planning and what actually happened. The calendar is where staff find a session, and the attendance sheet is where they record who came.

The calendar is also built to work on a phone as well as a large screen, because mentors often check it on the move.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

There are three views: month, week and agenda. A cohort filter narrows the calendar to one group. Each session shows its time, place, mentor and cohort. Each session links straight to its attendance sheet.
