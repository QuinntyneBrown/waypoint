# 041 · Viewing badges

## Welcome

Badges are how Waypoint marks progress along the way. This short video shows where a young person finds their badges, what an earned badge tells them, and how a locked badge shows what is still to come.

[pause 2s]

## Earned badges

Meet Amara. From her journey page she presses, view badge details, and arrives at a page called, Badges. Its subtitle says, every badge marks progress you have made.

At the top, a collection summary counts how many badges she has earned and how many are still to unlock. Under the heading, Earned, she finds her first badge, First Steps. The card says what it is for, complete your first lesson, and shows the date she earned it.

[pause 2s]

## Badges still to unlock

Below that, under the heading, still to unlock, are the badges she has not earned yet. Each locked card is clear about how to get there. The Code Starter badge says, complete every self-check in the welcome card lesson. A bar and a count show her progress toward it. Right now it says, zero of two.

That count is not a guess. It matches what she sees on her journey page and her home page, because they all come from one source.

[pause 2s]

## Milestones

At the bottom is a section called, your milestones. It is a timeline. It begins with the day she joined her cohort, and then lists the badge she earned, with its date. Underneath is a box that says, next up, with the Code Starter badge, its progress, and a label that says, current goal. So Amara always knows what she is working toward.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Earned badges show what they are for and the date they were earned. Locked badges show the criteria and a counted progress, such as zero of two. A timeline of milestones points to the current goal. Every count comes from a single source, so the numbers agree everywhere.
