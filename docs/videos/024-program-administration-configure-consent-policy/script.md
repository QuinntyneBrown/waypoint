# 024 · Setting the consent policy

## Welcome

Waypoint works with young people, so a guardian's permission matters. This video shows how Pat Morgan, the program administrator, sets the consent policy once, and how that policy then shapes what guardians see.

[pause 2s]

## The consent policy panel

Pat opens Organization settings, and finds the panel called Guardian consent policy. It says that these settings drive term access and youth media safeguards. There are three controls.

The first is the consent statement. This is the wording a guardian reads before giving permission. Pat writes it in plain language, for example, I give my child permission to take part in the Waypoint program this term.

The second is a box that says, Require re-consent each term. The note beside it explains the effect. A prior-term record will not unlock participation once a new active term begins.

The third is a box that says, Require media consent for youth media. Its note says that photos and video require a current applicable guardian permission.

[pause 2s]

## Saving the policy

Pat presses Save organization settings. Waypoint answers with a message that the settings are saved and recorded in the audit log. So there is always a trace of who changed the policy, and when. Only a program administrator can change these settings. Anyone else is turned away by the server, not just hidden on the screen.

[pause 2s]

## What the guardian sees

Now meet Ruth Clarke. Her child has asked Waypoint for guardian consent, and Ruth opens the secure one time link from her email. The page shows the statement exactly as Pat wrote it, under the heading Participation statement. Below it, in the optional media section, a note tells her the organization policy. Youth media requires recorded guardian permission. Participation consent stays separate from the optional media choices.

That separation is deliberate. A minor who has no participation consent on record is blocked from taking part, and is sent to get it. The policy Pat saved is what drives that flow.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

The consent statement, the re-consent rule and the media rule are set in one place. Saving them is recorded in the audit log, and only a program administrator can do it. Guardians see the statement and the media policy on the consent form. Without participation consent, a minor cannot take part.
