# 009 · Recovering a password

## Welcome

Forgetting a password is normal. This video follows one person from the forgot password page to a new password, and shows why the whole process is safe for everyone, including people who typed the wrong email.

[pause 2s]

## Asking for a link

Meet Amara. She cannot remember her password, so on the sign-in page she chooses forgot password. A page titled, reset your password, asks for one thing: her email. She types it and presses, send reset link.

The next page says, check your email. It tells her that if an account exists for her address, a link has been emailed. Her address is shown only in part, with the first letter and the domain, so a stranger looking over her shoulder learns very little. The link expires in one hour. If the email does not arrive, a button lets her send the link again.

Here is the careful part. If someone types an email that Waypoint has never seen, they get exactly the same page and exactly the same words. That way, nobody can use the reset page to find out who is in the program. The email is only sent when the account really exists.

[pause 2s]

## Choosing a new password

Amara opens the link from her email. The page says, set a new password. She types a new password, and types it a second time to confirm. The page shows the rule underneath: at least eight characters, including a letter and a number. When the password meets the rule and both boxes match, the reset password button becomes available.

She presses it.

[pause 2s]

## All set

Amara sees a page that says, your password was updated. It adds that, for her security, her other sessions were signed out. So if anyone else was signed in as Amara, they are out now. She presses sign in, and uses her new password.

[pause 2s]

## When the link does not work

A reset link works once, and for one hour. If Amara opens the same link a second time, or opens one that has expired, she sees a page that says, this link is invalid or expired. It offers a button to request a new link, and another to go back to sign in. Nothing else changes, and she can simply start again.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Anyone can ask for a reset, and the answer never reveals who has an account. The email address is shown only in part. Each link works once and expires after one hour. A new password must follow the rule, and both boxes must match. After a reset, other sessions are signed out.
