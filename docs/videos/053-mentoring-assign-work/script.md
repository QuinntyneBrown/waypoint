# 053 · Assigning work

## Welcome

A mentor does not only review work. A mentor also decides what a young person should do next. In Waypoint, a mentor can assign a lesson, a module, or a project to one youth, several youth, or a whole cohort. This video shows how that works, and what happens when the mentor confirms.

[pause 2s]

## Choosing what to assign

Grace opens Assign work. The first question is, what are you assigning? She picks Lesson. Then she chooses the item from the list. A type and an item are both required, so the form will not go further without them.

A summary panel on the right follows along. It shows the item she picked, how many youth are selected, and the due date.

[pause 2s]

## Choosing who and when

Next comes the question, who is this for. Grace can choose selected youth, or the whole cohort. She wants Amara to do this lesson, so she ticks Amara's name. Waypoint shows a small tag by the name if a youth is at risk, so a mentor can notice who might need extra support.

Then Grace sets a due date, and a reminder. She chooses to be reminded two days before it is due. The due date and the reminder are stored, and they drive the reminders and notifications Amara receives.

Last, there is an optional message to the youth. Grace writes, please complete this before our next session. A note under the box explains that each recipient receives the message in their mentor message thread.

[pause 2s]

## Review and confirm

Grace presses Review and assign. Nothing is created yet. A confirmation window asks her to check her choices. It names the item and the due date, says the work will be added to one youth journey, names who will receive it, says the message goes out right away, and says that a due reminder will be scheduled for each person.

Only when Grace presses the button that says Assign to one youth does Waypoint create the assignments. One record is made for each recipient, and the work appears in their journeys. Waypoint also reports how many youth received it, so the mentor knows it worked.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

A type and an item are required. The recipients are either selected youth or the whole cohort. A due date, a reminder, and a message are all available. Nothing happens until the mentor confirms. After confirming, each recipient gets the assignment in their journey, and the count is reported.
