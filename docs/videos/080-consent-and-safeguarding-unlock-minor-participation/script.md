# 080 · Unlocking a young person's participation

## Welcome

In Waypoint, a young person under the age of majority cannot start lessons until a parent or guardian has given permission. This video follows the whole journey, from the blocked screen, to the guardian signing, to the young person getting in.

[pause 2s]

## The blocked screen

Meet Imani Clarke. She has just joined the Fall cohort, and her mother, Ruth, has not signed the consent yet. Imani signs in. Instead of her home page she sees a screen headed, a guardian needs to sign off.

It explains, in plain words, that before she can start lessons, a parent or guardian needs to give participation consent, and that it only takes a minute. There are three buttons. Notify my guardian. My guardian can sign now. And Sign out.

Imani cannot reach lessons, quizzes, or submissions from here. Waypoint checks this on the server for every protected action, not only when she signs in.

[pause 2s]

## Asking the guardian

Imani presses Notify my guardian. A green message says that a secure consent link was sent to her guardian. Ruth receives an email with a secure, one-time link.

If Ruth is sitting beside Imani, Imani can choose My guardian can sign now instead. That signs Imani out and takes Ruth to the guardian sign in page, ready to open the consent page.

[pause 2s]

## The guardian signs

Ruth opens the link. She does not need an account for this form. The page names the young person, Imani Clarke, shows when the link expires, and states what the guardian is agreeing to.

Ruth types her full name and her relationship, Mother. She ticks the box that gives permission for Imani to take part. The media choices below it are optional, and Ruth leaves them alone. Then she types her full name again as her signature and submits.

A card says, consent recorded, and shows a reference number that starts with G C. A copy is sent to her email.

[pause 2s]

## Imani gets in

Imani does not need anyone to unlock her by hand. The next time she opens Waypoint, the check passes, and she lands on her youth home page, ready to begin.

In the same way, if a young person is marked as needing consent in the admin views, that mark clears by itself once consent is recorded.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

A minor without participation consent is held on one screen and cannot reach any learning. They can notify their guardian, or ask them to sign now. When the guardian signs, access opens with no further steps.
