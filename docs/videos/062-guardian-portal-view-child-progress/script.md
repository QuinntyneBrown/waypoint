# 062 · Following a young person's progress

## Welcome

A guardian wants to know how their young person is getting on, without reading private notes or lesson content. This video shows the read only view Waypoint gives guardians, and what it leaves out on purpose.

[pause 2s]

## Home at a glance

Ruth Clarke signs in as a guardian. She lands on a page called Your youth at a glance, with the name of her young person, Imani Clarke, underneath. Three tiles show overall progress, the attendance rate, and how many badges Imani has earned. Below them, a card called What's next shows the next session, and a button, View full progress.

[pause 2s]

## Modules and attendance

Ruth presses View full progress. The page is labelled Read only family view. At the top is a list of modules. Each one shows its status and how far through it Imani is, as a percentage.

Next comes the attendance history. Every session is listed with its date and a status, such as present or absent. Waypoint also supports late and excused, so Ruth sees the same words the mentors use.

[pause 2s]

## Quizzes, skills and badges

Further down are the approved quiz outcomes. A quiz only appears here once a mentor has approved it, and it shows the score and whether it was passed. Next to it are leadership competencies, with the level a mentor has assessed, such as proficient, and a percentage.

Then come the badges. Earned badges are listed with a star. Badges still to unlock are listed with an open circle, so Ruth can see what Imani is working towards.

[pause 2s]

## Schedule and shared updates

The upcoming schedule lists the next sessions with their dates and times. Last, a card called Shared mentor updates shows check-in notes, but only the ones the mentor chose to share. A check-in marked private never appears here.

There is also no button to edit anything. No lesson content, no quiz answers, no private reflections, and no private mentor notes are shown. And a guardian can only ever see their own linked young person.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Guardians get a read only view of progress, attendance, badges, and the schedule. Only approved quiz outcomes and shared mentor updates are shown. Nothing can be edited, and nothing private is visible.
