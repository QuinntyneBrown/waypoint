# 028 · Terms and tracks

## Welcome

A program runs in terms, and each term teaches from tracks of curriculum. This video shows how Pat Morgan, the program administrator, keeps terms and track availability up to date, and why pausing a track does not lose anyone's progress.

[pause 2s]

## Creating a term

Pat opens Organization settings and scrolls to the panel called Terms and cohort cycles. A note explains it. Maintain date ranges and advance each term through its lifecycle.

The form has four fields. A term name, a start date, an end date, and a term status. The status is a choice of three. Upcoming, Active, or Completed. Pat enters Fall, a start date in September, an end date in December, and chooses Upcoming. Then Pat presses Create term.

The term appears in the list below, with its dates and a coloured badge showing its status. If its details need to change, an Edit term button loads it back into the same form, ready to be saved again. As the term moves along, Pat can change its status from Upcoming to Active, and then to Completed.

[pause 2s]

## Pausing a track

Lower on the same page is the panel called Track availability. It lists every published track. A note explains what pausing does. Pausing a track preserves existing progress but blocks new enrolment and work assignment.

Each track has a badge, Enabled or Paused, and a button. For an enabled track the button says Pause track. Pat presses it, and the badge changes to Paused.

[pause 2s]

## What pausing means

Pausing is not deleting. A young person who is already working in that track keeps everything they have done. What stops is new activity. No new youth are enrolled into the track, and no new work is assigned in it. When the track is ready again, the same button now says Enable track, and one press brings it back.

[pause 2s]

## Who can change it

Terms and tracks are part of the organization settings. Only a program administrator can change them, and the changes to organization settings are recorded in the audit log.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

A term has a name, a date range, and a status of Upcoming, Active, or Completed. A track can be paused and enabled again. Pausing keeps all existing progress, and only blocks new enrolment and new assignments. Only a program administrator can make these changes.
