# 066 · Viewing your upcoming schedule

## Welcome

Volunteers give their time, so they should be able to plan it. This video shows the schedule page, where a volunteer sees every session they are assigned to help with. It follows Sam Whitfield, a volunteer, as Sam looks ahead.

[pause 2s]

## Opening the schedule

Sam opens the schedule from the volunteer menu. The page is headed your schedule, and a line underneath says, sessions you are assigned to help with.

Below that is a list of cards, one for each upcoming session. Sam has three. Each card shows the session's title, and a small badge at the right that gives its status. Here every badge says upcoming. Once attendance has been recorded for a session, its badge changes to show that. Under the title come the day and the start time, and how long the session lasts, ninety minutes. At the bottom of the card is the location, the main hall.

The sessions are listed in date order, the nearest first. Sam can see at a glance which day is next, and what the weeks ahead look like.

[pause 2s]

## Only your own sessions

The list shows only the sessions assigned to Sam. A session that belongs to another volunteer does not appear. Sessions are scheduled by program staff, who view them by cohort in calendar views. A volunteer sees the part of that calendar that is theirs, and nothing about the young people in it.

If a volunteer asks for anything outside that scope, Waypoint refuses the request. It is checked on the server every time.

[pause 2s]

## When nothing is scheduled yet

Now suppose a new volunteer signs in before anyone has assigned them to a session. The schedule page does not look broken. It shows a calm message, no upcoming sessions. It explains that no sessions are assigned yet, and that they will appear here once the volunteer is scheduled.

As soon as staff assign a session, it shows up on the schedule, and on the day itself it appears on the home page as today's session.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

The schedule lists every upcoming session assigned to the volunteer, with its title, day and time, length, location, and a status badge. It shows only the volunteer's own sessions, and never any youth information. When nothing is assigned, an empty message explains why, and new sessions appear as soon as staff schedule them.

That finishes the volunteer portal videos, which covered today's session, attendance, and the schedule.
