# 019 · The journey and impact on the home page

## Welcome

When someone first lands on the Waypoint home page, they want to see two things. What will happen to me if I join? And does this program really work? This short video shows the four step journey and the impact numbers on the home page.

[pause 2s]

## The home page

Ruth Clarke opens the public site. At the top, a headline says, raising up young leaders who love Jesus. Below it, a short paragraph says that the program pairs mentorship with real skills, in faith and in code. Two buttons, apply now and explore the programs, are right there.

[pause 2s]

## Your journey in four steps

Scrolling down, Ruth finds a section called, your journey in four steps. It has four cards, one for each step. Step one is Apply. Tell us about yourself and why you want to join. Step two is Join a cohort. Get matched to a neighbourhood cohort and a mentor. Step three is Learn the modules. Work through your track's modules with weekly support. And step four is Lead and graduate. Step into leadership, earn your badges, and graduate.

In a few lines, a young person can see the whole path from the first application to graduation.

[pause 2s]

## Growing leaders across the city

Next comes a section called, growing leaders across the city. It shows four numbers. How many learning tracks there are. How many cohorts. How many published modules. And how many neighbourhoods the cohorts are in. In this example, there are two tracks, two cohorts, four modules, and two neighbourhoods.

These numbers are counts of the program's own structure, taken from what is published. They are not counts of young people, and nothing on the page can point to any one of them. So the page shows the size of the program without sharing anything private.

Under the numbers, two buttons invite Ruth to start an application, or to get involved.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

The home page shows the four step journey, from apply to graduate. The impact numbers count tracks, cohorts, modules, and neighbourhoods, and never individual youth. Everything on the page is open to anyone, with no sign in.
