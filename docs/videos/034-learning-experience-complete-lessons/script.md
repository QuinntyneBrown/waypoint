# 034 · Reading and completing a lesson

## Welcome

Most lessons in Waypoint are for reading. This video shows what a youth sees in a lesson, how Waypoint remembers how they like to read, and what happens when they mark the lesson complete.

[pause 2s]

## The lesson page

Amara opens Living the Word from her curriculum. The page tells her this is step two of three, and that the lesson takes fifteen minutes.

She sees a scripture block, then a short section on what she will learn, then the teaching. At the end, a section called think about it asks, where can you practise neighbour love this week? Notes written for leaders are never shown to youth.

[pause 2s]

## Reading preferences

Above the scripture are three buttons for text size, and a bookmark button. Amara picks the larger text and bookmarks the lesson. A small message confirms, reading preferences saved. If she reloads the page, or comes back tomorrow, her text size and her bookmark are still there.

[pause 2s]

## When media does not load

Some lessons have optional media. If it fails to load, the lesson does not break. A message explains that the media could not be loaded and that the full lesson text remains available. A retry media button lets her try again. The scripture and the teaching stay readable the whole time.

[pause 2s]

## Marking it complete

At the bottom is the button, mark lesson complete. Waypoint asks her to confirm. Once she does, the lesson is recorded as complete with a time, what comes next in the curriculum unlocks, and she is taken straight to a reflection page. It shows the same question, and asks how she is feeling and what she thinks. That is the subject of another video.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

A lesson shows scripture, teaching and a reflection prompt, and never leader notes. Text size and bookmark are remembered. If media fails, the text still works and she can retry. Marking complete unlocks what is next and leads to the reflection.
