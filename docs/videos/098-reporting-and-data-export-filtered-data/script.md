# 098 · Exporting filtered data

## Welcome

Sometimes a report on the screen is not enough. A board meeting wants a spreadsheet, or a funder wants a formatted summary. This video shows how program staff export data from Waypoint, and why an export never contains more than it should.

[pause 2s]

## Exporting what you see

Pat Morgan, a program administrator, is looking at the reports hub. Pat has filtered it to one cohort, its track, and its lead mentor, Grace Adeyemi. The page says one youth record.

At the top right there are two buttons. Export CSV, and Export PDF. Pat presses Export CSV. For a moment the button says, preparing CSV. Then the file downloads, and a green message appears. CSV export prepared for the current report and filters.

That message is the promise. The export covers exactly the report on the screen and exactly the filters Pat chose. Nothing from outside that scope is included. A CSV gives raw rows, ready for a spreadsheet. A PDF gives a formatted summary, ready to print or share.

[pause 2s]

## One report, two formats

Next Pat opens the youth report for David Osei, and presses Export PDF. Again a file downloads, and again the message confirms it. This time it says, PDF export prepared for the current report and filters. The export follows whichever view is open, so the youth report gives a youth file, and the overview gives an overview file.

Other places in the portal work the same way. The progress board and the attendance history have their own export buttons, and each exports what is on the screen.

[pause 2s]

## Safe by design

Exports are not a side door. A person can only export data their role is allowed to see. A mentor's export covers only their own youth.

Every export is also written to the audit log. Pat opens the audit log, filters by Pat's own name and the action called data exported, and finds the entries, with who exported and when.

The audit log itself is more sensitive. Only program administrators, and director level roles, can export it. When Grace tries to open the audit log, Waypoint turns her away.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

An export matches the screen and the filters, exactly. A CSV is raw rows, and a PDF is a formatted summary. Nobody can export more than their role can see. Every export is recorded in the audit log, and the audit log export is limited to administrators.
