# 086 · Editing your profile

## Welcome

Everyone in Waypoint has a profile. It holds a display name, an email address, pronouns, and an optional photo. This video shows how a person keeps those details up to date, and how Waypoint protects them.

[pause 2s]

## The profile form

Meet Amara. She opens her settings, and the profile tab comes first. She sees four things. A display name, marked as required. An email address, also required. Pronouns, which are optional, with a hint that shows what to type. And a profile photo.

Her current name and email are already filled in, so she changes only what she wants to change.

[pause 2s]

## Checks before saving

Amara types her pronouns. Then, by mistake, she puts something that is not an email address into the email field, and presses, save profile. The page marks the field in red and says, enter a valid email address. Name and email are required, and the email has to look like one.

[pause 2s]

## The photo

The photo field says, JPG or PNG, up to five megabytes. Stored privately. If Amara picks a file that is not really a picture, Waypoint refuses it. The message says, file content does not match a valid JPG or PNG image. The check looks at what is inside the file, not only at its name, and that protects the program from files that are not what they claim to be.

A real JPG or PNG is accepted, shown as a small preview, and can be removed again with a button. The photo is stored privately, so it is not open to anyone who has the link.

[pause 2s]

## Saving

She fixes the email and presses save profile. A short message appears. Profile updated.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

The profile holds a display name, an email, pronouns and a photo. Name and email are required, and the email has to be valid. Photos must be a real JPG or PNG, up to five megabytes, and are stored privately. A short message confirms every save.
