# 030 · A person's record

## Welcome

Behind every row in the directory is a person. This video walks through a young person's record as Pat Morgan, the program administrator, sees it, and explains what it shows, and what it deliberately leaves out.

[pause 2s]

## The tabs

Pat opens a youth's record. At the top is the person's name, their cohort and role, and a badge showing that the account is Active. Below that is a row of six tabs. Profile. Guardian and consent. Progress. Attendance. Notes. And Account.

The Profile tab shows the basics. Email address, mentor, cohort, pronouns, whether onboarding is complete, and the date the person joined.

[pause 2s]

## Guardian and consent

The next tab, Guardian and consent, matters most for a minor. On the left is the guardian contact. Name, relationship, email, phone number, and an emergency contact. On the right is the consent record. A badge says Valid. Under it, whether participation consent is recorded, whether media consent was granted, when it was signed, and until when it stays valid.

At a glance, Pat can tell whether a young person is clear to take part, and who to call.

[pause 2s]

## Progress and attendance

The Progress tab shows how many lessons the youth has finished, in words and as a bar. In this example, one of two lessons is complete, which is fifty percent.

The Attendance tab shows how many sessions the youth attended out of the sessions held, a percentage, and a list of those sessions with Present or Absent and the date.

[pause 2s]

## Notes and privacy

The Notes tab lists operational check-in summaries. The type of meeting, the date, the mentor, a short outcome, and a follow-up date if there is one. A line at the top tells the reader what is never here. Private prayer requests are never included. So the administrator's view never exposes them.

The last tab, Account, holds the account actions. Those are the subject of another video.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

One record, six tabs. Profile, Guardian and consent, Progress, Attendance, Notes, and Account. Consent status and guardian contact are on the same record. Check-in notes are summaries only, and private prayer requests are never shown.
