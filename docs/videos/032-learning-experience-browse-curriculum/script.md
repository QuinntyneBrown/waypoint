# 032 · Browsing the curriculum

## Welcome

Every young person in Waypoint follows a track of modules. This video shows how a youth finds their place in that track, what is open to them, and what is not open yet.

[pause 2s]

## The curriculum page

Meet Amara. She opens Curriculum from the side menu. At the top she sees her tracks, and below them the modules of the track she has chosen.

Each module has a name, a short description, and a percentage that shows how far she has come. Inside a module, the lessons are listed in order, each with a small status. Her first lesson is a coding lesson, and it says done. The next lesson says not started. Only published modules appear here, so Amara never sees work that staff are still drafting.

[pause 2s]

## Gating

Now look at the module quiz. It is marked locked, and it tells her why. Complete all lessons in this module. Waypoint shows the rule instead of leaving her to guess.

The same idea applies between modules. The second module is locked, and a note tells her to complete the first module. A locked module cannot be opened by tapping it. And this is not only about what the screen shows. The server checks the order too, so a locked lesson cannot be completed by sending a direct request.

[pause 2s]

## More than one track

Some youth are enrolled in more than one track. Amara can switch between them with the buttons above the modules, and the page shows the curriculum of the track she picked.

If a track has no published modules yet, the page does not fail. It says, this track is not set up yet, and explains that published modules will appear there when they are ready.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Curriculum shows only published modules, in order. Each module shows how far along the youth is. Quizzes and later modules stay locked until the earlier work is done, and the reason is always on the screen. The server enforces the same order. A track with nothing published shows a clear empty message.
