# 023 · Assigning mentors

## Welcome

Every young person in Waypoint works alongside a mentor. This video shows how Pat Morgan, the program administrator, decides who mentors whom, and how Pat keeps the workload fair.

[pause 2s]

## Pairing a youth with a mentor

Pat opens Mentor assignments from the menu. The page has two panels side by side. The first one is called Pair a youth. Pat picks a young person from the list. Each choice also shows the youth's cohort and current mentor, or the word Unassigned. Then Pat picks a mentor. Every mentor in that list shows how many youth they already carry. Pat presses Assign mentor.

Waypoint saves the pairing, and says so at the top of the page. The change is visible on the mentor's roster straight away. When Grace Adeyemi signs in, the young person is already on her list, and there is nothing more for Pat to do.

[pause 2s]

## Watching the load

Further down the page, every active mentor has a card. A card shows the mentor's name, how many youth they carry, how many check-ins they have done, and how many reviews. A badge compares the load with a target band, and the page states the band in plain words. Four to eight active youth.

Below the band, the badge says Under target. Inside it, Balanced. Above it, Overloaded, and an overloaded mentor's card also carries a warning to rebalance. After Pat's pairing, Grace's card shows one youth, because the numbers follow the change as soon as it is saved. This is how Pat spots a mentor who is stretched, or one who has room, before it becomes a problem.

[pause 2s]

## Cohort mentor teams

The second panel is the Cohort mentor team. Pat chooses a cohort, then sets one lead mentor. Next, Pat ticks any number of supporting mentors. The lead mentor cannot also be ticked as a supporting mentor, so nobody is counted twice. Pat presses Save cohort team, and Waypoint confirms with a message at the top of the page.

The roles belong to the cohort, not to the person. So Grace can be the lead mentor in one cohort, and a supporting mentor in another. Setting her up in one cohort does not change her role in the other.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Pairing a youth with a mentor takes effect at once, and shows on the mentor's roster. Each mentor's card shows youth, check-ins and reviews against a target band, and an overloaded mentor is flagged. A cohort has one lead and any number of supporting mentors, and a mentor's role can differ from one cohort to the next.
