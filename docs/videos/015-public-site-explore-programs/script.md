# 015 · Exploring the programs

## Welcome

A young person, or a guardian, wants to know what Waypoint actually teaches. This short video shows the public programs page, where anyone can read about the learning tracks and the mentorship that comes with them.

[pause 2s]

## The two tracks

Ruth Clarke is a guardian, and she opens the public site. She chooses Programs in the top menu. The page is titled, two tracks, one mission. It says that every young leader grows through mentorship and hands-on learning.

Below the title there is one card for each published track. In this example there are two, Bible and Leadership, and Coding for Kingdom. Each card has the name of the track and a short description of what it is about.

[pause 2s]

## Modules and badges

Inside each card is a numbered list of modules, in the order a young person works through them. Next to a module, a small green label names the outcome badge the module leads to. In this example, Servant Leadership leads to the Servant Heart badge, and Web Foundations leads to the First Build badge.

So Ruth can see, before anyone applies, what a young person works on and what they can earn.

Only published tracks and published modules appear on this page. Anything still being prepared stays out of public view. If nothing has been published yet, the page says, programs are being prepared.

[pause 2s]

## Mentorship

Further down, a section called, you're never on your own, explains the mentorship model. Every youth is paired with a mentor who walks alongside them through the program. Three cards say how. Weekly check-ins. Reflection reviews. And extra support when it's needed.

Under that, an apply now button lets Ruth start an application whenever she is ready.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

The programs page is open to everyone. Each published track shows its modules and their outcome badges. Unpublished content never appears. The mentorship model is explained on the same page, with an apply now button close by.
