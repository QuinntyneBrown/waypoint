# 007 · Setting up a new youth

## Welcome

When a young person joins Waypoint, the first thing they see is a short setup. It takes three steps, none of them hard, and it can be skipped. This video follows Amara Bell through it, right after she accepts her invitation.

[pause 2s]

## Step one, your profile

The first step is called, tell us about you. Her display name is already filled in from the invitation, and a star marks it as the one required answer. Below it is a box for pronouns, where the page suggests examples such as she and her, or they and them. Amara types hers.

Then there is a place for a profile photo. It is optional. The page says it can be a JPG or PNG, up to five megabytes, and that it is stored privately. Amara leaves the photo empty and presses next.

[pause 2s]

## Step two, your cohort

Step two is called, your cohort. Amara does not choose anything here. Her cohort and mentor were set by the program team when they sent her invitation, so the page simply confirms it. It says she is placed in a cohort, and that her mentor will guide her journey.

If a youth has not been placed yet, this step says so instead. It explains that cohorts are assigned by the program team, and that content unlocks once they are placed.

[pause 2s]

## Step three, notifications

The last step is notifications. Waypoint starts with sensible defaults. Lesson reminders are on. Mentor messages are on. Announcements are off. Amara can flip any of these switches now, and the page reminds her she can change them anytime in settings. She presses finish.

[pause 2s]

## Landing at home

Amara arrives on her youth home page, which welcomes her by name. Her setup is done.

Suppose she had pressed skip for now, at any step. That works too. She would land on the same home page, and the setup can be picked up again later. Nobody is held back from their program by a form.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Onboarding has three steps: profile, cohort, and notifications. Only the display name is required. Cohort and mentor are set by staff, and the youth only sees them confirmed. Notification defaults are lessons and mentor messages on, announcements off. Skip for now leads to the home page.
