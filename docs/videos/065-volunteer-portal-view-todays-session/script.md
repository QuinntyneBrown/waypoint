# 065 · Viewing today's session

## Welcome

When a volunteer signs in, they should know in a few seconds where to be and what to do. This video shows the volunteer home page, called today's session, and follows Sam Whitfield, a volunteer, on a day when Sam has a session and on a day when Sam does not.

[pause 2s]

## Landing on today

Sam signs in. Waypoint takes volunteers straight to their own home page, the same way it sends a mentor or a youth to theirs. The page is headed today's session, and a line underneath says, your assignment for today, at a glance.

A single card shows the session. It has the session's title, the day and the start time, and how long it lasts, ninety minutes in this case. Under that is the location, the main hall. Then comes one more line, three on the roster. That is a head count, and nothing else. The card does not list the young people's names or anything about them.

[pause 2s]

## What a volunteer does not see

The home page shows only the session Sam is assigned to today. It does not show other volunteers' sessions, a cohort's progress, or any youth records. A volunteer who asks for something outside that scope is refused with a forbidden message, because Waypoint checks every request on the server, not only on the screen.

This keeps the page light, and it keeps young people's information with the people who are meant to hold it.

[pause 2s]

## Going to attendance

At the bottom of the card is a single green button, take attendance. It opens the roster for this session, where Sam marks each young person present or absent. That step has its own video.

[pause 2s]

## A day with no session

Now suppose Sam signs in on a day without an assignment. The page does not show an error or a blank screen. It says, no session today. It explains that Sam has no assigned session today, and suggests checking the schedule for what is coming up. A button, view schedule, takes Sam there.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Volunteers land on today's session. One card shows the title, time, length, location, and a head count of the roster, and no names. A single button leads to attendance. Volunteers see only the session assigned to them. On a day without a session, a clear message points to the schedule.
