# 043 · Writing a reflection

## Welcome

After a lesson, Waypoint invites a young person to stop and think. This short video shows the reflection page, how a draft is kept safe, and how the young person decides who reads what they write.

[pause 2s]

## The reflection page

Meet Amara. She has just finished a lesson, and Waypoint opens a page headed, Reflect on Living the Word. Under the title it says, take your time. You can save this as a private draft.

A box called, think about it, asks a question from the lesson. Where can you practise neighbour-love this week? Below it, she is asked, how are you feeling? She can pick hopeful, thoughtful, unsure, or challenged. Then there is a large box for, your reflection, and a smaller one called, one action I will take, which is optional.

[pause 2s]

## What is required

Suppose Amara presses submit with nothing written. The page tells her what is missing. It says, reflection text is required, and it asks her to choose how she is feeling. A note by the text box explains the rule, required when you submit, drafts can be unfinished. So she can save a half-written thought, but she cannot submit one.

[pause 2s]

## Who can read it

Near the bottom there is a box called, share with my mentor. It is off by default. The note says, when it is off, this reflection is private to you. Turn it on only if you want your assigned mentor to read it and respond during check-ins. The choice is hers, one reflection at a time.

[pause 2s]

## Drafts and leaving the page

Amara writes a few lines and chooses thoughtful. Then she decides to leave. Waypoint warns her that she has unsaved content, and lets her save it as a draft before she goes.

When she comes back, a message says, draft restored. Her words and her feeling are exactly where she left them, and sharing is still off. When she is ready, she presses submit reflection, and the page says, reflection submitted.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

A reflection needs a feeling and some text to submit. The optional action is just that, optional. Sharing with a mentor is off unless the youth turns it on. Leaving with unsaved words brings a warning, and a saved draft is restored when the youth returns.
