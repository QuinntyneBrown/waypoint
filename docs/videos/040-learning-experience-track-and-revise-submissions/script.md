# 040 · Tracking and revising a submission

## Welcome

After a young person sends in their work, the next question is, what happens now? This short video follows one submission through review, through a request for changes, and on to approval.

[pause 2s]

## Under review

Meet Amara. She has just submitted her assignment, the Neighbour Love project. Waypoint takes her to the submission status page. The title shows a small label that says, submitted. Under it is a copy of exactly what she sent, and a timeline. The timeline reads, submitted, then under review. Her work is now read-only, so what the mentor reads is what she sent.

[pause 2s]

## When changes are needed

Grace, her mentor, reviews the work and asks for a revision. Now Amara's label says, needs revision. The timeline has moved on, and a new box called, mentor feedback, shows Grace's note. It says, add the scripture that guided your action. Below it is a button, revise and resubmit. A change in status also sends Amara a notification.

[pause 2s]

## Revising

Amara presses the button. The revise page shows Grace's feedback at the top, and her earlier answer is already filled in, so she does not start from nothing. She updates the text to mention Luke ten, verse twenty-seven, and presses, resubmit assignment.

[pause 2s]

## Back under review

Waypoint returns her to the status page, and the label is back to under review. The timeline keeps the whole story. Submitted, under review, needs revision, resubmitted, and under review again. Nothing is hidden or overwritten.

[pause 2s]

## Approved

When Grace approves, the label says, approved. Amara sees Grace's comment, a rubric result, nine of ten points, and any badge the approval unlocks. Here, it is the Practical Love badge.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

A submission becomes a read-only copy. The timeline shows every step, including revisions. A request for changes comes with the mentor's note and a way back to resubmit. Approval shows the points, the comment, and any badge unlocked.
