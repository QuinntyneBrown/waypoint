# 070 · Receiving notifications

## Welcome

Things happen in Waypoint all day: a mentor sends a message, work gets graded, a session is coming up. This video shows how each person is told about it, and how one tap takes them to the right place.

[pause 2s]

## A live notification

Meet Amara Bell, a youth in the program. She opens her notifications page. It has a small badge that says, live updates on. That means Waypoint is connected, and new items will appear without a refresh.

While Amara is looking at the page, her mentor, Grace Adeyemi, sends her a message. A moment later, a new item appears at the top of Amara's list. Its title is, new message. Its text says, you have a new message. A small label marks it as new, and the page also says, new notification.

[pause 2s]

## Read state

Unread items stand out from the ones Amara has already seen. At the top right there is a button, mark all read. Amara presses it, and every new label clears at once.

If Amara was not connected when the message arrived, nothing is lost. The notification simply waits for her, and appears the next time she opens the page.

[pause 2s]

## One tap to the right place

Each notification carries a link to the place it is about. Amara opens the new message notification, and Waypoint takes her straight to her messages page, where Grace's note is waiting. She does not have to look for it.

Waypoint creates notifications for events like graded work, a badge earned, a new message, something due soon, a session reminder, an at-risk alert, or an approval that is needed. Each goes to the right people, with a title, a body and a link.

[pause 2s]

## Choosing how to hear

Not everyone wants every notice in every way. Amara's settings include a notifications tab. For each kind of notice, such as lesson reminders, mentor messages, session reminders and work graded, she can choose in-app, email, or both. Waypoint respects those choices when it creates notifications.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Notifications arrive live while you are connected, and wait for you if you are not. Unread items are marked new, and mark all read clears them. Each one links to the screen it is about. Your notification settings decide how you are told.
