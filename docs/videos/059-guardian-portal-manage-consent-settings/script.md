# 059 · Managing consent settings

## Welcome

Waypoint asks a parent or guardian for permission before a young person takes part. This video shows where a guardian can see that permission, change the optional media choices, and withdraw consent if they need to.

[pause 2s]

## Participation consent

Meet Ruth Clarke. She is the guardian of Imani, a young person in the Fall cohort. Ruth signs in and opens the Consent page from the menu on the left.

On the right she sees a card called Participation consent. It carries a green badge that says Active. Under the badge are the facts of what she signed. Who signed, and how they are related to the young person. The date and time. The method, which is a typed e-signature. The date the consent is valid until. And a reference number that starts with the word consent.

This part is read only. Ruth cannot edit a consent she has already given. She can only look at it, ask for a copy, or withdraw it.

[pause 2s]

## Media choices

Below that card is a second card called Media consent. Media means photos and video of Imani. This permission is optional, and it is separate from participation.

Ruth ticks Allow media use. Then she chooses what that covers. There are three options. All photos and video. Group photos only. And first-name captions. Ruth picks group photos only, and first-name captions. She leaves all photos and video empty.

She presses Save media consent. A green message says, media consent saved. Each option is stored on its own, so Waypoint knows exactly what she agreed to.

[pause 2s]

## Withdrawing consent

If Ruth changes her mind about taking part, she can press Withdraw consent. Because this is serious, Waypoint asks her to confirm first.

Once she confirms, the badge turns red and says Withdrawn. The Withdraw button disappears, and the record stays on the page so the history is clear. From that moment, the permissions that depended on that consent are revoked going forward. A young person who is a minor is then held on the consent needed screen until a guardian gives consent again.

A guardian can only ever see and change their own record. Another family's details are never shown.

[pause 2s]

## Things to remember

Participation consent is shown read only, with who signed, when, how, and a reference number. Media consent is optional, and each choice is saved on its own. Withdrawing needs a confirmation, and it takes effect from then on.
