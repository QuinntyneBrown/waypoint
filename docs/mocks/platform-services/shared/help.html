Getting started

What is Waypoint?
Waypoint is a program that helps youth across Toronto grow in the Word and in leadership, one lesson at a time. You'll work through modules like Servant Leadership with your mentor, Marcus Bello.
Where do I find this week's lesson?
Open Home to see what's next, or browse everything under Curriculum.

Lessons & quizzes

How do I submit my work?
Open your lesson, type your reflection at the bottom, and tap the green Submit button. Your mentor sees it right away and sends feedback.
Can I retake a quiz?
Yes. Quizzes are about learning, not grades — you can retake any quiz as many times as you like.

Account

How do I change my password?
Go to Settings › Profile and tap Change password.
How do I turn off notifications?
Manage email and push preferences under Settings › Notifications, including quiet hours.
