Help

How can we help?

Quick answers to the things youth ask most.

Stuck on something?
Your mentor Marcus Bello is one message away.
How do I submit my work?
  1. Open your lesson from Lessons.
  2. Type your reflection in the box at the bottom.
  3. Tap the green Submit button.
Your mentor sees it right away and will send you feedback.
How do I take a quiz?
When a lesson has a quiz, you’ll see a Start quiz button. Tap one answer per question, then tap Finish. You can retake a quiz any time — it’s about learning, not grades.
I forgot my password
No worries. On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and we’ll email you a reset link. Still stuck? Message your mentor and they’ll help.
How do I earn badges?
You earn badges by finishing lessons, showing up, and serving your community. Check Badges to see what you’ve unlocked and what’s next.
Can I use Waypoint offline?
Yes. Lessons you’ve opened stay available offline, and anything you submit will send once you’re back online.
