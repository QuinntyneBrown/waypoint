The heart of it
“For even the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many.”Mark 10:45

What to do

  1. Look for one person who needs a hand this week — at home, school, or church.
  2. Serve them without being asked, and without expecting anything back.
  3. Snap a photo (of the moment, the result, or a related object — no faces needed).
  4. Write 3–5 sentences about what you did and how it changed you.
