Last Shall Be First

Mark 10 · about 12 min

“For even the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many.”Mark 10:45

James and John wanted the best seats — one on Jesus’ right, one on his left. It’s an honest ask. Most of us want to be seen, to matter, to lead. Jesus doesn’t shut that desire down. He redirects it.

In the world, greatness flows downhill: the more important you are, the more people serve you. Jesus flips it. “Whoever wants to become great among you must be your servant.” The path up runs through the towel and the basin, not the throne.

That’s the shape of Word Up leadership. Being first isn’t about status — it’s about who you’re willing to put first. This week, watch for one person you can quietly serve without being asked.
