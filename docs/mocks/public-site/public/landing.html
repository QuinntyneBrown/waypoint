Toronto youth leadership

Raising up young leaders who love Jesus.

Waypoint trains youth across Rexdale, Warden and St. Clair in the Bible, real leadership and practical coding — then walks with them through mentorship that lasts.

What we do

Three pillars, one journey

Every young person moves through a cohort built on Scripture, skills and relationship.

Bible & Leadership

Who Is Jesus?, Identity in Christ and Servant Leadership — youth learn the Word and how to lead a small group of their own.

See the track

Coding for Kingdom

From Intro to Scratch to Web Basics: HTML & CSS, youth build real projects and discover tech as a way to serve their neighbourhood.

See the track

Mentorship

Every youth is paired with a trained mentor for weekly check-ins, encouragement and prayer — no one grows alone.
