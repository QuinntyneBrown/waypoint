Privacy & consent

Privacy, consent & youth safety

Last updated July 2026. Waypoint works with minors, so we take privacy and safety seriously. This page explains, in plain language, how we handle information and keep youth safe.

Working with minors
A parent or guardian must consent before any youth under 18 takes part in Waypoint.
1 · What we collect

Information we collect

When someone applies or is referred, we collect the youth's name and age, guardian contact details, neighbourhood, track interest, and why they want to join. Volunteers and partners share their own contact details. We only collect what we need to run the program and stay in touch.

2 · How we use it

How we use your information

  • To place youth in a cohort and match them with a mentor.
  • To contact guardians about progress, attendance and events.
  • To keep youth safe and follow up when someone needs support.
  • To understand our impact across Rexdale, Warden and St. Clair.

We do not sell personal information, and we never share it for advertising.

3 · Youth safety

Keeping youth safe

Every mentor and volunteer working with youth completes a screening and reference check. Mentors meet youth in supervised settings, and we follow safe-ministry practices for one-to-one contact. If you have a safety concern, contact Director Shawn Peters through our contact page right away.

4 · Media consent

Photos & media

We sometimes take photos or video at sessions and showcase nights to share stories of what God is doing. We only publish a young person's image with guardian consent, and you can withdraw that consent at any time by contacting us. Media consent is separate from program consent — you can say yes to one and no to the other.

5 · Storing & protecting data

How we protect information

Application and program information is kept in access-controlled systems used only by Waypoint staff and assigned mentors. We keep records only as long as needed to run the program and meet our obligations, then remove them.

6 · Your choices

Your rights & choices

  • Ask what information we hold about you or your youth.
  • Correct anything that's out of date.
  • Withdraw consent and ask us to remove records.

To make any of these requests, reach out through the contact page.

7 · Contact

Questions?

Waypoint · Toronto, Ontario · Director: Shawn Peters. Email hello@waypoint.example or use our contact page. We're glad to walk through any of this with you.
