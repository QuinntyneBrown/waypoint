About Waypoint

We build young leaders who love Jesus and their city.

Waypoint is a Toronto youth-leadership program. We take young people from Rexdale, Warden and St. Clair and disciple them through the Bible, leadership training, coding and one-to-one mentorship.

Our mission

To raise up a generation of young leaders who are rooted in Scripture, confident in their identity in Christ, and equipped with real skills to serve their neighbourhoods.

Our heart

Servant leadership, modelled on Jesus. We meet youth where they are, walk with them, and send them back to lead the very blocks they came from.

How it works

A cohort you move through together

Each cohort runs one term. Youth meet weekly, work through modules, and are supported by a mentor the whole way.

1 · Apply or get referred

A youth applies for themselves, or a guardian or leader refers them.

2 · Join a cohort

Placed in a neighbourhood cohort like Rexdale Fall '25 with a lead mentor.

3 · Learn the modules

Work weekly through Bible & Leadership and Coding for Kingdom modules.

4 · Lead & graduate

Youth lead a small group, earn badges, and graduate ready to serve.
