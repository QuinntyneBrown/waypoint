# Word Up Platform — PRD

## Purpose

Word Up is a youth-development platform combining learning, mentorship, cohort participation, and program operations. It replaces fragmented spreadsheets and messaging with one system for tracking each learner’s journey.

## Users

The platform serves youth, mentors, program administrators, guardians, volunteers, and public visitors. Role-based access must limit every user to appropriate information and actions.

## Core Requirements

- Deliver Bible and leadership learning alongside coding lessons, quizzes, assignments, reflections, progression, and badges.
- Support mentor check-ins, feedback, attendance, risk flags, youth assessments, and cohort communication.
- Enable administrators to manage applications, people, roles, cohorts, curriculum, schedules, announcements, reporting, and imports.
- Give guardians read-only progress and schedule visibility plus consent and contact management.
- Give volunteers only assigned-session, schedule, and attendance access.
- Provide public program information and application, contact, volunteer, and donation pathways.
- Enforce guardian consent, safeguarding controls, audit history, privacy, and scoped authorization.
- Support responsive use, accessibility, notifications, offline attendance, and secure data handling.

## Success Measures

Success means higher lesson completion, consistent mentor engagement, accurate attendance, timely follow-up for at-risk youth, reduced administration, and complete consent records. The system must be reliable, understandable, and safe.
