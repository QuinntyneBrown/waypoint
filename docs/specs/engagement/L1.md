# L1 — Engagement & Operations (ENGAGE)

High-level requirements for sessions, attendance, RSVP, announcements, messaging, and notifications.

## L1-ENGAGE-001: Sessions & calendar
The system must let staff schedule sessions, events, and retreats (title, cohort, mentor, date, time, location, weekly recurrence) and view them in month/week/agenda calendar views per cohort.

## L1-ENGAGE-002: Attendance tracking
The system must let mentors and volunteers record per-youth attendance (Present/Late/Excused/Absent) for a session, with bulk mark, running tallies, unsaved-changes warnings, per-session notes, and offline capture that syncs on reconnect; and let directors view/edit attendance on a mentor's behalf and review attendance history and rates.

## L1-ENGAGE-003: RSVP
The system must let youth RSVP to sessions and events (going / maybe / can't make it, with an optional note to the mentor) and set reminders.

## L1-ENGAGE-004: Announcements
The system must let staff compose announcements targeted to an audience (whole org, a cohort, and/or a role such as Youth, Guardians, Mentors), send now or schedule for later, and track read rates; recipients see them with per-item read/unread state.

## L1-ENGAGE-005: Messaging
The system must provide 1:1 threaded messaging between youth and their mentor (and relevant staff), plus a mentor "broadcast to my group" that each youth receives as a personal message.

## L1-ENGAGE-006: Notifications
The system must generate an actionable notification feed per user for relevant events (graded work, badges earned, new messages, due dates, session reminders, at-risk alerts, approvals needed), each deep-linking to the relevant screen, with read/unread state and mark-all-read.
