# L1 — Learning Experience (LEARN)

High-level requirements for the youth learner experience.

## L1-LEARN-001: Curriculum delivery & progression gating
The system must present youth with their track's modules and lessons, enforce sequential gating (later lessons, quizzes, and modules unlock only after prerequisites complete), and let youth switch between the tracks they are enrolled in.

## L1-LEARN-002: Lesson delivery
The system must deliver a lesson-reading experience with scripture blocks, teaching text, adjustable reading text size, optional media, bookmarking, step progress, and a "mark complete" action that routes the youth into the post-lesson reflection.

## L1-LEARN-003: Coding-track delivery
The system must deliver interactive coding lessons (stepwise content, code samples, an in-lesson editor/preview, a self-check checklist) and coding projects that youth submit for mentor review.

## L1-LEARN-004: Quizzes — attempts, scoring, results
The system must let youth take quizzes one question at a time with autosaving/resumable answers, submit under a confirmation that answers are then immutable for that attempt, auto-score against the quiz's pass mark within the allowed attempts, and show a result with pass/fail, score, per-question review of correct vs. chosen answers, and any earned badge.

## L1-LEARN-005: Assignment submission & review lifecycle
The system must let youth submit assignment work (photo/text/link per the assignment's allowed types, with an attestation), track it through a review lifecycle (Submitted → Under review → Approved or Needs revision), show mentor feedback and rubric outcome, and let youth resubmit when a revision is requested.

## L1-LEARN-006: Reflections
The system must let youth write post-lesson reflections (free text plus a mood/engagement check-in), save drafts, and choose per-reflection whether to keep it private or share it with their mentor, defaulting to private.

## L1-LEARN-007: Badges & journey view
The system must show youth their journey roadmap (modules complete/in-progress/locked), earned vs. locked badges with unlock criteria and counted progress, and milestone history.

## L1-LEARN-008: Learner progress self-view
The system must give youth a personal progress view with overall progress, quiz average and history, lessons completed, attendance record, and leadership-competency levels.
