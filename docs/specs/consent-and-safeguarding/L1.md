# L1 — Consent & Safeguarding (CONSENT)

High-level requirements for guardian consent, minor safeguarding, and the accountability audit trail.

## L1-CONSENT-001: Guardian consent capture & enforcement
The system must require guardian consent before a minor (under 18) participates, capturing participation consent (required) separately from photo/media consent (optional, granular, withdrawable), recording a typed e-signature with signer, timestamp, validity period, and reference, and blocking program access for minors who lack participation consent.

## L1-CONSENT-002: Youth-safety audit trail
The system must maintain a tamper-evident, append-only audit log of who did what, when, and from where, for accountability and safeguarding, while keeping sensitive check-in content private/redacted from the log view.

## L1-CONSENT-003: Minor data protection & safeguarding controls
The system must apply safeguarding controls specific to serving minors — consent-gated participation and media use, retained mentor–youth contact records visible to the director, and role assignment that does not by itself widen youth-record access.
