# L1 — Mentoring & Coaching (MENTOR)

High-level requirements for the mentor/coach experience.

## L1-MENTOR-001: Mentor dashboard & roster
The system must give mentors a dashboard summarizing their assigned youth, pending reviews, average progress, and at-risk counts, plus a searchable/filterable roster of their youth with per-youth status.

## L1-MENTOR-002: Youth detail record (mentor view)
The system must give a mentor a full per-youth record — profile, guardian contact, at-risk banner, progress, attendance, check-in history, submissions, and competencies — scoped to youth the mentor is assigned to.

## L1-MENTOR-003: Submission grading
The system must let mentors grade a youth's assignment submission against its rubric and either approve it, request a revision (with a mandatory note and optional guardian notification), or save a draft.

## L1-MENTOR-004: Quiz assessment approval & override
The system must auto-score quizzes but require a mentor to approve (or override, e.g. for partial credit) each result before it counts, with youth seeing only the approved score, and support bulk approval.

## L1-MENTOR-005: Competency assessment
The system must let mentors assess each youth across defined competencies using an ordered level scale (Not started → Developing → Proficient → Mastered) with notes, and track a summary of mastery.

## L1-MENTOR-006: Check-ins
The system must let mentors log 1:1/call/session check-ins capturing engagement/mood, goals discussed, prayer requests, action items, and a next follow-up date, with a private-by-default visibility toggle (prayer requests stay mentor-only unless shared) and optional guardian-informed flag, and view a check-in history/journal.

## L1-MENTOR-007: At-risk flags
The system must surface youth who have gone quiet or fallen behind as at-risk flags driven by signals (inactivity, low progress, missed sessions), and let mentors raise, act on, and resolve (unflag with a reason) them, visible to the director.

## L1-MENTOR-008: Assign work
The system must let mentors assign a lesson, module, or project to selected youth or a whole cohort, with a due date, reminder cadence, and an accompanying in-app message, confirmed before sending.
