# L1 — Guardian Portal (GUARDIAN)

High-level requirements for the parent/guardian portal.

## L1-GUARDIAN-001: Guardian portal
The system must give a guardian a read-only view of their child's aggregate progress, module completion, attendance, badges, and schedule; cohort/org announcements; and the ability to manage their own contact info and their media/participation consent — never the child's lesson content, quiz answers, private reflections, or account fields.
