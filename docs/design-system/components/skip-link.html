Overview

Hidden until focused. Required on every page with a top bar or sidebar.

Anatomy

  1. LinkInk pill, appears top-left on focus.

Variants

One variant.

Sizes

One size.

States

Pointer and focus states are forced with data-state so they can be reviewed statically; real pages get them from :hover, :focus-visible, :active and ARIA attributes.
