Spinner

An indeterminate loading indicator for short waits inside a region.

WCAG 2.2 AALight + dark

Overview

Use when content will arrive in under a few seconds and its shape is unknown. Prefer skeletons for page content and the button's busy state for actions.

Anatomy

  1. Ring2px border, accent arc.
  2. TextVisible or visually hidden.
