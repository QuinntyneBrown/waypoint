Layout

One mobile-first grid governs columns, margins, gutters and page widths.

Extracted 25 October 2026Light + dark contrast verified

Principles

Margin is viewport-to-content space; gutter is column-to-column space. The content container caps at 80rem. Staff pages compose header, filters, counts, records and pagination one gutter apart. Guardian reading flows cap at 36rem; setup flows cap at 60rem.
