Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA is a component contract and a screen-level responsibility.

Extracted 25 October 2026Light + dark contrast verified

Principles

Use native controls, visible labels, logical headings, landmarks and a first-focus skip link. Normal text needs 4.5:1; large text and meaningful graphic edges need 3:1. The 2px focus ring must remain visible and unobscured. Keyboard and touch can complete the same tasks; do not require dragging. Respect reduced motion and forced colours. Language is declared on the document and changed phrases.

Group choices with fieldset and legend. Identify errors in text and suggest a correction. Focus a linked error summary after failed submission; do not erase input. Important errors persist. Modal focus is contained and restored. Do not block paste, password managers or accessible authentication. Reuse previously entered data, keep Help in the same place, and require review for financial or destructive actions.

Static checks validate structure and declared contrast; they do not certify screen-reader behavior. Future Angular components and screens need keyboard, screen-reader and axe checks, including status announcements and focus restoration.
