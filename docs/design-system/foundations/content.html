Speak calmly and directly to staff and families, in their language.

Principles

Use sentence case and specific verbs. Say “Send permission slip”, not “Submit”; “Add a child”, not “Create entity”. An error states what happened and how to recover. An empty state names what is missing and offers one action. Avoid blame and jargon. Keep medical details out of shared previews.
