Colour

Violet identifies primary actions; semantic status colours always carry written meaning.

Extracted 25 October 2026Light + dark contrast verified

Principles

Preserve the inherited violet ramp and both theme palettes. Default, muted and subtle text are separate roles; disabled text and decorative dividers are not evidence of readable text contrast. Medical flags pair danger text with a tinted surface. All declared pairs below are checked in both themes.
