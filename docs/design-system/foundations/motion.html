Motion

Movement explains change and remains optional.

Extracted 25 October 2026Light + dark contrast verified

Principles

Use 120ms for hover, 200ms for reveals, 320ms for sheets and 500ms only for deliberate completion. Tooltips wait 300ms before showing and close immediately. Reduced-motion tokens reduce transitions to near-zero; decorative loops stop. Never flash or animate critical medical information.
