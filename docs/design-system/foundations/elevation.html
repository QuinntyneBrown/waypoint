Foundation

Elevation

A deliberately flat system: hairline borders do most of the work; four shadows for things that float.

WCAG 2.2 AALight + dark
  • Overview
  • Shadows
  • Surfaces
  • Z-order
  • Tokens

Overview

The mocks used only two shadows (--w-shadow-sm, --w-shadow-pop). They map to levels 1 and 3; levels 2 and 4 fill the gaps for dropdown cards and dialogs.

Shadows

--shadow-1

Surfaces

  • Canvas → surface → raised. In light, canvas and surface are both white and separated by hairlines; raised adds a shadow.
  • In dark, each level is lighter (ink-950 → ink-900 → ink-850), so elevation reads even where shadows disappear.

Z-order
