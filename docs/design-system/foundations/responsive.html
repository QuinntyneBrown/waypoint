Responsive

Let content wrap and controls stay reachable from a 360px phone to an office laptop.

Extracted 25 October 2026Light + dark contrast verified

Principles

At 640px related short fields can share a row and sheets can center. At 768px primary top-bar navigation replaces the phone tab bar. At 1024px secondary staff navigation appears. Search waits until 1280px; content does not widen beyond 80rem. Long names and Spanish copy wrap. Tables scroll inside named regions. Dialog footers stay reachable and fixed navigation includes safe-area padding.

Test 200% text zoom and a 320 CSS-pixel effective viewport. Never hide required labels or medical details to make a layout fit.
